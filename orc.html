The Blood Road

A hub-by-hub Orc leveling route from the Valley of Trials to level 60, with the class quests for Warrior, Hunter, Rogue, Shaman and Warlock folded into the steps, and a placeholder for the Forever-only Orc Mage. Pick your class once and the page shows only yours. Built on Classic Era rules; everything WoW Forever is known to change is flagged in violet.

Start here

This is a route, not an encyclopedia. Each bracket tells you where to bind your hearthstone, which flight point to grab, and a numbered order of quests to run. Tick the boxes as you go; the page remembers them in this browser only.

Steps shaded in red are class quests. Choose your class in Pick your class and the other classes' steps disappear; the choice is remembered, and ?class=warlock in the address bar does the same thing. With no class chosen every class step shows, each with a tag.

Two kinds of callouts appear throughout. Era is the verified Classic Era baseline. Forever marks what WoW Forever has publicly changed or added, and what to re-check once the beta is playable. Where a coordinate or count comes from memory rather than a checked source it is tagged verify; treat those as "the right area", not gospel.

47,60 style numbers are map coordinates for the zone named in that step. Quest names are in bold. Coordinates were checked for Durotar, the Barrens and the class chains; elsewhere they are close enough to find the NPC with the map open.

The route stays on Kalimdor as long as the quests hold out, because an Orc's hearth, trainers and flight net all sit between Orgrimmar and the Crossroads. Eastern Kingdoms legs start at 30 (Stranglethorn) and are reached through the zeppelin tower outside Orgrimmar's main gate, never through Undercity unless a flight point is the reason.

Pick your class

Solo leveling speed, 1–60
Show quests for

Showing every class quest. Pick one and the other classes' steps are hidden everywhere on the page; the Evergreen addon does the same in-game from your character's class.

Hunter Fastest

A pet from level 10 and nothing ever touches you again, and Command makes it hit 5% harder than any other race's. The Razor Hill taming chain sends you at a Dire Mottled Boar, a Surf Crawler and an Armored Scorpid, then to Ormak Grimshot in Orgrimmar for Beast Training. Keep the pet fed, keep arrows stocked, and let Hardiness cover the one time something stuns you.

Warlock Fast / safest

Imp at 1 from Ruzan in the valley, Voidwalker at 10 from Gan'rul Bloodeye in the Cleft of Shadow, and from then on the Voidwalker tanks while you drain. Command's 5% goes on the pet here too. Succubus at 20 and the Orb of Soran'ruk at 25 are optional detours; the Felhunter at 30 and the free Felsteed at 40 are not. Never sits to drink: Life Tap, then Drain Life.

Shaman Fast

Totems arrive by quest, not by trainer: Earth at 4 from Canaga Earthcaller, Fire at 10 from Searn Firewarder, Water at 20, Air at 30. Ghost Wolf at 20 is a 40% mount you already own, Windfury at 30 with a two-handed axe and Axe Specialization is the best leveling weapon combination in the game, and Healing Wave means you never sit to eat.

Rogue Medium

Fast kills, no downtime tools until First Aid, and every class quest is an errand: Blade of Cunning at 10 through Therzok in the Cleft, lockpicking practice on the Tide Razor at 16–18, Poisons through Shenthul at 20 with a group elite at the end. Blood Fury before every opener; Hardiness is the reason Orc rogues win duels on PvP realms.

Warrior Slowest

Gear-dependent and downtime-heavy, but Blood Fury, Hardiness and Axe Specialization are three racials a warrior wants. Stances come by quest: Defensive at 10 (Veteran Uzzek at Far Watch Post), Berserker at 30 (Fray Island) alongside the Whirlwind weapon chain. Run Wailing Caverns and Razorfen Kraul for weapons, buy Heavy Linen Bandages by the stack, take the axe every time.

Mage Forever Unpublished

Orc Mage is a new WoW Forever combination; Era has no such class. Mai'ah in the Den already trains Troll mages, so the starting spot is known, but no Orc mage class quest has been published and the beta cap is 30. The Horde chains that exist in Era (the Sen'jin Ju-Ju Heaps errand, Deino's Xavian robe at 26, Tabetha's wand at 30–40, Xylem's Sunken Temple chain at 50) are the likely template; they appear below tagged Forever and verify.

Forever

Orc keeps its five Era classes and adds Mage. The other new combinations go to other races (Undead paladin, Dwarf shaman, Troll warlock, Human hunter, Gnome priest). Every talent tree is reworked, so the speed ranking above is Era's and may reshuffle, and Command's removal narrows the hunter and warlock lead.

Rules of the road

Hearthstone policy

  • Bind it where the page tells you, and nowhere else. Every bracket has one hearth and one flight point to collect.
  • Orgrimmar is the best-connected hearth in the Horde: the zeppelin tower to Undercity and Grom'gol stands right outside the gate. Bind in Razor Hill while Durotar is the route, at the Crossroads and Camp Taurajo for the Barrens; from 25 on bind at the hub you are questing from and keep Orgrimmar as the flight hub.
  • Log out in an inn or a capital. Rested XP doubles kill experience; a night's log-off is worth a bar and a half early on.

Kill order

  • Kill everything green or yellow on the way to an objective. Red is downtime, grey is nothing.
  • Turn in quests in batches. Walk the hub once per loop, not once per quest.
  • Skip escorts that fail on a bad pull, group quests you cannot solo two levels late, and anything that sends you across an ocean for a single turn-in.

Orc-specific habits

  • Bind a key to Blood Fury on day one. In Era it halves healing received for 25 s, so press it as you open a pull, never at low health with a bandage in hand. Warlocks and hunters: it does nothing for the pet, so it is a pure emergency melee button for you.
  • Learn First Aid in Razor Hill (Rawrk, by the inn) verify. Linen from the Kul Tiras humans at Tiragarde pays for it; bandages are your only emergency button until 20.
  • Skinning plus Herbalism from Razor Hill onward, or Mining if you plan Engineering at 60. Orcs have no gathering racial, so pick for gold: skins and herbs both sell from level 6.
  • Class quests are in the steps, shaded and tagged. Do them when they appear: each one is a spell or pet you cannot buy, and the level-10 ones (stance, pet, Fire totem, Voidwalker) are the single biggest speed jump in the route.

Gold

  • Sell everything grey. Vendor whites that are not your weapon upgrade.
  • Mount money at 40 is 100 gold: 20 for Apprentice Riding from Kildar and 80 for a wolf from Ogunaro Wolfrunner, both in the Valley of Honor in Orgrimmar. Shamans have Ghost Wolf at 20 and warlocks a free Felsteed at 40, so both can spend the gold on weapons first.
  • Buy weapons, not armor. A green axe two levels above yours is the one purchase that pays back.
1–6

Valley of Trials

HubThe Den 42,68 (Durotar, south-west valley)
HearthAlready bound to the Valley of Trials. No inn; nothing to do.
Flight pointNone. Durotar has no flight master outside Orgrimmar in Era.
TrainersAll stand in or beside the Den: Frang (warrior), Jen'shan (hunter), Rawg (rogue) verify, Shikrik (shaman), Nartok (warlock); Mai'ah (mage, Forever)
Before you move

Turn on Auto Loot in the Interface options. Bind a key to Blood Fury. Read the class scroll you spawn with: it is a free turn-in at your trainer. The valley is one loop: the Den, the boar farms north and east, the scorpid field north-west, the Burning Blade cave in the north wall, back to the Den. Vendors Zlagk and Duokna stand north of the Den entrance.

Leave at 6 with both deliveries in the log. East out of the valley; the road forks, and Sen'jin Village is the south-east branch.

Forever

Nothing announced touches the valley. Orc mages start here with Mai'ah in the Den, who already trains Troll mages; no Orc mage quest has been published. Blood Fury with no healing penalty is usable from level 1 without a second thought.

First stop

Far Watch Post is the first thing on the far bank: Kargal Battlescar takes Conscript of the Horde and gives Crossroads Conscription (Sergra Darkthorn, 910 XP for a walk), Ak'Zeloth takes his letter and gives The Demon Seed, and Uzzek waits under his tree to the south-west if you are a warrior. The road runs west then bends south to the Crossroads. Shamans stop at Grol'dom Farm on the way. You never need Camp Taurajo's Journey to the Crossroads; that is the Tauren entry. On a PvP realm the Crossroads is the most contested town in the game; quest out of Camp Taurajo whenever it is on fire.

Leave at 20. Fly Camp Taurajo → Sun Rock Retreat and rebind there.

Forever

The 15–25 Undercity-surface zone is the Forsaken's answer to a crowded Barrens; for an Orc it is one zeppelin from the Orgrimmar gate, so on a PvP realm where the Crossroads burns nightly it is worth the trip. Shen'Dralas is confirmed to sit between Mulgore and Desolace with no published level range; if it turns out to be 15–25 it would slot in here instead.

Two ways to run it

Stranglethorn has the most quests of any zone but they spread from 30 to 45. Do the 30–35 layer now (Nesingwary's first two tiers, Grom'gol's troll war, Booty Bay's coastal jobs), go to Desolace for 32–35, and return to Stranglethorn at 37 and again at 42 as the higher tiers open. Leave at 35. Fly Shadowprey → Orgrimmar, zeppelin to Undercity, fly to Tarren Mill, road east into Arathi. Rebind at Hammerfall. This is the one leg that runs through Undercity: Hammerfall has no flight point until you have walked to it.

Orgrimmar logistics

Getting around the city

  • One main gate, on the south side facing the Durotar road. The zeppelin tower stands just outside it. Inside, the city is five valleys joined by the Drag.
  • Valley of Strength (inside the gate): bank, auction house, the inn (Innkeeper Gryshka), flight master Doras on the upper tier verify, and Zankaja on the path up toward the Valley of Wisdom.
  • Valley of Honor (north-east): the Hall of the Brave (warrior trainers Grezz Ragefist, Sorek, Zel'mak 80,31), the Hunter's Hall on its west side (Ormak Grimshot 66,18, Sian'dur, Xor'juul, pet trainer Xao'tsu), the Arms of Legend weapon masters Hanashi and Sayoc 81,20, and the mounts: Kildar (riding) and Ogunaro Wolfrunner (wolves).
  • Valley of Wisdom (north-west): Grommash Hold with Thrall, Nazgrel, and the shaman trainers (Kardris Dreamseeker, Sagorne Creststrider, Sian'tsu, and Searn Firewarder for every Call of quest). Not the Valley of Spirits, whatever the name suggests.
  • Valley of Spirits (south-west): the Spirit Lodge (priest trainers) and the Darkbriar Lodge (mage trainers Uthel'nay, Deino, Enyo; Thuul sells portals). Forever Orc mages train here.
  • Cleft of Shadow (the cave under the Drag): the Shadowswift Brotherhood rogues (Shenthul 43,54, Ormok, Gest, Therzok), the Darkfire Enclave warlocks (Gan'rul Bloodeye 47,50, Zevrost, Grol'dar, Kurgul), Neeru Fireblade's tent, Kor'ghan, and the Ragefire Chasm entrance.
  • Professions cluster in the Drag; First Aid is in Razor Hill if you missed it verify.

Zeppelins and boats

  • The tower outside the main gate has two platforms: Undercity and Grom'gol. The flight master is inside the gate, so Orgrimmar is the only Horde city where a flight and a zeppelin are two minutes apart.
  • Ratchet ↔ Booty Bay boat links the Barrens to Stranglethorn without a zeppelin.
  • Undercity's tower returns to Orgrimmar and Grom'gol; you use it for Kargath, Stonard, Revantusk and the Plaguelands flights only. Thunder Bluff has no zeppelin and nothing you need until 60.

Class quest stops

  • Levels 1–4: warlock Imp from Ruzan and shaman Call of Earth from Canaga Earthcaller, both at the Den in the Valley of Trials.
  • Level 10: every class. Warrior to Uzzek at Far Watch Post (from Tarshaw Jaggedscar, Razor Hill); hunter to Thotar in Razor Hill, then Ormak Grimshot in the Hunter's Hall; shaman Call of Fire from Searn Firewarder in Grommash Hold; warlock Gan'rul's Summons from Ophek in Razor Hill to Gan'rul Bloodeye in the Cleft; rogue Therzok in the Cleft (from Kaplak, Razor Hill) verify.
  • Levels 16–18: rogue lockpicking practice, Wrenix in Ratchet and the Tide Razor verify.
  • Level 20: shaman Call of Water (Searn → Islen Waterseer south of Ratchet); warrior Speak with Ruga at Camp Taurajo; rogue Poisons from Shenthul in the Cleft; warlock Succubus from Cazul and Zankaja.
  • Level 25: warlock Orb of Soran'ruk from Doan Karhan, southern Barrens.
  • Level 30: shaman Call of Air (Prate Cloudseer, Thousand Needles); warrior The Islander (Fray Island) then the Whirlwind chain from Bath'rah in Alterac; warlock Seeking Strahad in Ratchet for the Felhunter.
  • Level 40: warrior Whirlwind Weapon at Bath'rah; warlock Felsteed from Strahad in Ratchet.
  • Level 50: every class has a Sunken Temple chain from its Orgrimmar trainer: warrior Fallen Hero (Swamp of Sorrows), hunter Ogtinc (Azshara), shaman Bath'rah (Alterac), rogue Ravenholdt then Xylem (Hillsbrad, Azshara), warlock Impsy (Felwood), and for Forever mages Xylem (Azshara).
  • Level 60: warlock Dreadsteed from Mor'zul Bloodbringer in Burning Steppes.

Mount and gold

  • Apprentice Riding from Kildar and a Dire, Timber or Brown Wolf from Ogunaro Wolfrunner, both in the Valley of Honor in Orgrimmar. Together 100 gold at level 40 before reputation discounts, and the vendor is on the way to every trainer you use.
  • Shamans have Ghost Wolf at 20 (40% speed, usable indoors, instant with the talent). Warlocks get the Felsteed free from Strahad Farsan at 40 and pay only for the riding skill verify. Neither replaces a wolf at 60 for a shaman, but both let you spend the gold on weapons first.
  • Realistic gold by 40 on this route with Herbalism plus Skinning and nothing bought but weapons: 130–170. Without gathering: 70–90, which is why the 38–40 bracket sits in Dustwallow, where the dragonkin drop vendor greens.
  • Epic Swift wolf at 60: 1,000 gold, 900 with Orgrimmar Exalted. Every Durotar quest and most of the Crossroads and Ratchet-side Barrens quests count toward that rep; Camp Taurajo's go to Thunder Bluff. Warlocks: the Dreadsteed chain costs around 150 gold of materials and a Dire Maul West run instead.

WoW Forever watchlist

Confirmed as of Sep 19, 2026

World of Warcraft: Forever is Blizzard's permanent Classic+ ruleset: the original continents with new zones, dungeons, raids and quests, a talent rework, and a level cap that stays at 60. Beta runs Sep 17 to Oct 21, 2026 (level 20 cap, rising to 30); launch is Nov 4, 2026. Everything below is from the BlizzCon reveal and beta datamining; treat it as a list of things to re-check in the client, not settled fact.
