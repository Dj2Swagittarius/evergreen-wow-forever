Start here

This is a route, not an encyclopedia. Each bracket tells you where to bind your hearthstone, which flight point to grab, and a numbered order of quests to run. Tick the boxes as you go; the page remembers them in this browser only.

Steps shaded in pink are class quests. Choose your class in Pick your class and the other classes' steps disappear; the choice is remembered, and ?class=mage in the address bar does the same thing. With no class chosen every class step shows, each with a tag. Priest is a Forever-only Gnome class: its button is there, but its low-level quests are unpublished and only the faction-wide Sunken Temple chain is listed.

Two kinds of callouts appear throughout. Era is the verified Classic Era baseline. Forever marks what WoW Forever has publicly changed or added, and what to re-check once the beta is playable. Where a coordinate or count comes from memory rather than a checked source it is tagged verify; treat those as "the right area", not gospel.

47,53 style numbers are map coordinates for the zone named in that step. Quest names are in bold. Coordinates were checked for Dun Morogh, Loch Modan and the class chains; elsewhere they are close enough to find the NPC with the map open.

A Gnome shares a starting valley, a hub village and a capital with the Dwarves, and the first twenty levels never leave the snow and the loch. From 20 on the route is the Alliance one: Duskwood and the Wetlands, the Menethil boats to Kalimdor, then the same 30–60 zones every Alliance guide uses. Ironforge is the hearth of record: the Deeprun Tram makes Stormwind a free minute away, so every "go to Stormwind" below is a Tram ride, and every Kalimdor leg leaves from Menethil Harbor, one flight north of the gate.

Pick your class

Solo leveling speed, 1–60
Show quests for

Showing every class quest. Pick one and the other classes' steps are hidden everywhere on the page; the Evergreen addon does the same in-game from your character's class.

Warlock Fastest

An Imp at 4 from the troll cave below Anvilmar, a Voidwalker at 10 from the Slaughtered Lamb in Stormwind (a Tram ride, the longest level-10 errand any Gnome runs), and from then on the pet tanks while you Drain Life and never sit. Expansive Mind pads the Life Tap pool. Succubus at 20 through the Barrens and Ashenvale, Felhunter at 30 from Strahad in Ratchet, a free Felsteed at 40, and Escape Artist for the day the Voidwalker loses aggro.

Mage Fast

The Gnome class. Free food and water, Frost Nova plus Frostbolt kiting, Escape Artist as a second escape and Expansive Mind on top. Bink in the Hall of Mysteries hands you the Gizmonitor errand at 10 for a starter staff or orb, the Manaweave Robe at 15 runs through the Blue Recluse and Silver Stream Mine (your own loch), the Astral Knot Garment at 26, then Tabetha's wand chain at 30–40 and the Celestial Orb at 40.

Rogue Fast

Ambush, Sinister Strike, and no weapon racial to plan around: buy whatever green is best. Blade of Cunning at 10 from Hulfdan Blackbeard in the Forlorn Cavern, by way of Onin MacHammar outside Gnomeregan; lockpicking practice at 16 at Alther's Mill in Redridge; Poisons at 20 through Klaven's Tower. Escape Artist beats every net and snare a rogue used to have to Vanish from, and Ravenholdt starts at 24 on the way past Southshore.

Warrior Slowest

Gear-dependent and downtime-heavy, with no weapon racial to help. Defensive Stance at 10 by bringing Muren Stormpike a troll's head from below Frostmane Hold, then Tormus Deepforge's Grey Iron weapon from Ironband's Compound; Fire Hardened armor pieces at 28–30; Berserker Stance at 30 on Fray Island and the Whirlwind weapon chain at 35–40. Escape Artist gives a warrior what it never had: a way out of a snare to reach the caster. Run the Deadmines and Gnomeregan for weapons, buy bandages by the stack.

Priest Forever Medium, unpublished

New Gnome and class combination in WoW Forever. Two racial priest spells are announced, Confounding Flash (a confuse on up to five enemies) and Contingency Plan (an ally save in the Guardian Spirit mould), but no trainer placement, no level and no quest giver has been documented from the beta. Everything before the level-50 Sunken Temple chain is unpublished. Treat the button as a placeholder until the client is playable. verify

Forever

Gnomes keep these four classes in Forever and gain Priest; the other new combinations go elsewhere (Undead paladin, Dwarf shaman, Human hunter, Night Elf mage, Troll warlock, Orc mage). Every talent tree is reworked, so the speed ranking above is Era's and may reshuffle. Fear Ward becomes baseline for every priest, and a Gnome priest's two racial spells are on top of that.

Rules of the road

Hearthstone policy

  • Bind it where the page tells you, and nowhere else. Every bracket has one hearth and one flight point to collect.
  • Ironforge is the centre of the northern Alliance flight net and the Deeprun Tram makes Stormwind part of it. Bind in the field hub you are questing from; hearth to Ironforge only when a class quest or a trainer visit is due, then fly back out.
  • Log out in an inn or a capital. Rested XP doubles kill experience; a night's log-off is worth a bar and a half early on.

Kill order

  • Kill everything green or yellow on the way to an objective. Red is downtime, grey is nothing.
  • Turn in quests in batches. Walk the hub once per loop, not once per quest.
  • Skip escorts that fail on a bad pull, group quests you cannot solo two levels late, and anything that sends you across an ocean for a single turn-in.

Gnome-specific habits

  • Bind Escape Artist next to your hearthstone key and use it the moment a net or a root lands. Its half-second cast is the only thing between a Gnome and a Defias Trapper's second net; do not save it for something worse.
  • Learn First Aid in Kharanos from the physician in the Thunderbrew Distillery 47,53 verify. Linen from the troggs and the Frostmane pays for it; bandages are your only emergency button until 20.
  • Engineering plus Mining, from level 10. Engineering Specialization is the racial nobody else has: learn it from Springspindle Fizzlegear in Tinker Town verify, take Mining from Yarr Hammerstone at Steelgrill's Depot verify, and Dun Morogh and Loch Modan are copper and tin from end to end. Skinning plus Mining is the gold-first alternative; the racial is wasted on it.
  • Class quests are in the steps, shaded and tagged. Do them when they appear: each one is a spell, pet or weapon you cannot buy, and the level-10 ones (Defensive Stance, Voidwalker, Blade of Cunning, Bink's staff) are the single biggest speed jump in the route. A Gnome's are all in Ironforge or Dun Morogh except the warlock's, which is a Tram ride to Stormwind.

Gold

  • Sell everything grey. Vendor whites that are not your weapon upgrade.
  • Mount money at 40 is 100 gold: 20 for Apprentice Riding from Binjy Featherwhistle and 80 for a Blue, Green or Red Mechanostrider verify from Milli Featherwhistle, both at Steelgrill's Depot outside Kharanos. Warlocks get the Felsteed free at 40 and can spend the 100 gold on weapons.
  • Buy weapons, not armor. A green weapon two levels above yours is the one purchase that pays back. No Gnome weapon racial exists, so buy the best green regardless of type.

Before you move

Turn on Auto Loot in the Interface options. The class rune you spawn with (Simple Rune, Encrypted Rune, Glyphic Memorandum or Tainted Memorandum) is a free turn-in at your trainer inside Anvilmar. You are a Gnomeregan refugee in a Dwarf valley: Felix Whindlebolt inside the hall is the first of your own people you meet, and his lost luggage is the first Gnome quest. Vendor and repair by the door 30,72. The valley is two loops: the trogg field east of the hall and the troll cave in the south.

Leave at 5 with Senir's Observations and Supplies to Tannok in the log; kill the troggs in the pass so you hit 6 in Kharanos.

Forever

Nothing announced touches Coldridge. A Gnome Priest starts here in Forever; expect a priest trainer at the back of Anvilmar beside the mage and warlock, and an In Favor of the Light breadcrumb to Kharanos at 4 like the Dwarf's. None of it is published. verify

Two ways to run it

Stranglethorn has the most quests of any zone but they spread from 30 to 45. Do the Shimmering Flats first (flat ground, no travel, 30–32), then Stranglethorn's 30–35 layer (Nesingwary's first tiers, the Rebel Camp, Booty Bay's coastal jobs), Hillsbrad at 34–35, and Desolace whenever the jungle gets thin. Buy food and water in Booty Bay before you leave it; the jungle sells nothing between camps.

Leave at 35. Fly Southshore, ride north over the Thandol Span into Arathi, or fly Menethil → Refuge Pointe once you have it. Refuge Pointe has no inn in Era: keep the hearth at Southshore.

Forever

Nothing announced touches Stranglethorn, Thousand Needles or Desolace for the Alliance. Shen'Dralas, the new elven-ruins zone, opens off the Valley of Bones in southern Desolace with no published level range; if it is 30–35 it replaces the Desolace swing and Nijel's Point is the door.

Ironforge logistics

Getting around the city

  • The Commons (inside the gate): the bank, the auction house 25,74, the Stonefire Tavern (Innkeeper Firebrew) for the bind, and the ring road that reaches every other district in under a minute on foot.
  • The Great Forge (centre): Gryth Thurden the gryphon master 55,48, Tormus Deepforge the warrior's smith 49,43, the Ironforge Physician for First Aid 55,58 verify, and the High Seat 39,58 where The Reports and the Uldaman chain end.
  • Mystic Ward (north-west): the Hall of Mysteries holds the mage trainers and Bink 27,8, the priest trainers (High Priest Rohan, Toldren Deepiron, where a Forever Gnome priest will train verify) and the paladin trainers; Talvash del Kissel for the Uldaman necklace, and the wand vendor 23,16.
  • Military Ward (east): the Hall of Arms with the warrior trainers and Muren Stormpike 71,90, the hunter trainers, and Timberline Arms for weapon skills 62,89.
  • Forlorn Cavern (north): the rogue trainers and Hulfdan Blackbeard 52,15, the warlock trainers and demon trainer 48,9, the fishing trainer on the pool.
  • Tinker Town (north-east): High Tinker Mekkatorque and the Gnomeregan Exiles, Tinkmaster Overspark, Gnoarn, the Engineering trainers, and the Deeprun Tram platform. This is the Gnome quarter; every Gnomeregan dungeon quest and every Gnome NPC in the city stands here.
  • Hall of Explorers (east): Prospector Stormpike, Librarian Mae Paledust, Roetten Stonehammer, Historian Karnik and the Uldaman turn-ins. Professions cluster in the Great Forge and the Commons.

Tram and boats

  • The Deeprun Tram runs Tinker Town ↔ the Dwarven District, Stormwind, free and about a minute. Every "go to Stormwind" in this guide means the Tram: the Slaughtered Lamb, SI:7, the Wizard's Sanctum, the Cathedral, Furen Longbeard and Brohann Caskbelly in the Dwarven District at the far end of the platform.
  • Menethil Harbor (Wetlands) is the Alliance port and it is one flight from Ironforge: the Bravery to Auberdine (Darkshore) and the Lady Mehley to Theramore (Dustwallow). Fly Ironforge → Menethil, or Thelsamar → Menethil once you have both.
  • Auberdine ↔ Rut'theran (the Moonspray) reaches Darnassus; Booty Bay ↔ Ratchet (the Maiden's Fancy, neutral) links Stranglethorn to the Barrens for the warlock's Strahad trips. Stormwind Harbor does not exist in Era.

Mount and gold

  • Apprentice Riding from Binjy Featherwhistle and the Blue, Green or Red Mechanostrider verify from Milli Featherwhistle, both at Steelgrill's Depot outside Kharanos, Dun Morogh 49,48, not in Ironforge. Together 100 gold at level 40 before reputation discounts; the discount is Gnomeregan Exiles reputation, which every Dun Morogh, Loch Modan and Gnomeregan quest feeds.
  • Warlocks learn Summon Felsteed from Strahad Farsan at 40, free; only the 20-gold riding lesson is left to pay, and it still comes from Binjy. Warlocks spend the mount money on weapons.
  • Realistic gold by 40 on this route with Engineering plus Mining and nothing bought but weapons and Engineering materials: 90–130, because Engineering eats what Mining earns. With Skinning plus Mining and no Engineering: 130–170. Either way the 38–40 leg sits in Dustwallow and Stranglethorn, where the humanoids drop cloth and vendor greens.
  • Epic Swift Green, White or Yellow Mechanostrider at 60 verify: 1,000 gold, 900 with Gnomeregan Exiles Exalted. The Icy Blue Mechanostrider Mod A is the fourth epic colour and costs the same.
