Classic WoW · Alliance · Night Elf · 1–60

The Moon Road

A hub-by-hub Night Elf leveling route from Shadowglen to level 60, with the class quests for Warrior, Hunter, Rogue, Priest and Druid folded into the steps: Bear Form, the three tamings, the Blade of Cunning, Starshards and Vorlus Vilehoof all at 10, Cure Poison and Aquatic Form in Darkshore. Pick your class once and the page shows only yours. Built on Classic Era rules; everything WoW Forever is known to change is flagged in violet.

Start here

This is a route, not an encyclopedia. Each bracket tells you where to bind your hearthstone, which flight point to grab, and a numbered order of quests to run. Tick the boxes as you go; the page remembers them in this browser only.

Steps shaded in indigo are class quests. Choose your class in Pick your class and the other classes' steps disappear; the choice is remembered, and ?class=druid in the address bar does the same thing. With no class chosen every class step shows, each with a tag. Night Elf mage is a Forever-only combination and is not on this page; when Blizzard publishes its class quests it gets a button.

Two kinds of callouts appear throughout. Era is the verified Classic Era baseline. Forever marks what WoW Forever has publicly changed or added, and what to re-check once the beta is playable. Where a coordinate or count comes from memory rather than a checked source it is tagged verify; treat those as "the right area", not gospel.

47,60 style numbers are map coordinates for the zone named in that step. Quest names are in bold. Coordinates were checked for Teldrassil, Darkshore and the class chains; elsewhere they are close enough to find the NPC with the map open.

The route stays on Kalimdor to 23: Teldrassil, Darkshore and Ashenvale sit in a line, and Darnassus is a free hippogryph ride from Auberdine. Then it crosses to the Eastern Kingdoms on the Bravery (Auberdine to Menethil Harbor) for the Wetlands, Duskwood and Hillsbrad, and from 30 on it follows the shared Alliance route with the boats doing the work. Ironforge and Stormwind have every trainer a Night Elf needs, so from 25 on "your capital" in a step means whichever of the three is closest.

Pick your class

Solo leveling speed, 1–60
Show quests for

Showing every class quest. Pick one and the other classes' steps are hidden everywhere on the page; the Evergreen addon does the same in-game from your character's class.

Hunter Fastest

A pet from level 10 and nothing ever touches you again. Dazalar in Dolanaar sends you at three Teldrassil beasts (a Webwood Lurker, a Nightsaber Stalker and a Strigid Screecher), then to Jocaste in Darnassus for Tame Beast. Keep the pet fed, keep arrows stocked, and Shadowmeld covers the one patrol you could not outrun. Nightsabers and owls are the tameable pets of the starting forest, and the Rake is not yours; Darkshore's Moonstalkers are.

Rogue Fast

Ambush, Sinister Strike, and a racial that stacks with stealth. Blade of Cunning at 10 from Syurna in Darnassus (Sethir's journal at the Oracle Glade), lockpicking practice at 16 from Lucius in Lakeshire on the first Eastern Kingdoms leg, Poisons at 20 through Klaven's Tower on the Westfall border. Bandages and Evasion cover the pulls that go wrong; in Forever, Shadowmeld ends them.

Druid Medium, then fast

Slow until Bear Form at 10 (a Moonglade trip, then Lunaclaw in a cave east of Auberdine), steady until Cat Form at 20, then fast: Cat kills, Bear tanks the extra pull, Aquatic Form at 16 swims the Darkshore and Wetlands water quests, Travel Form at 30 is a mount you own ten levels early. Teleport: Moonglade makes every druid quest a short trip. Night Elf is the only Alliance druid in Era; Forever adds Skyborne.

Priest Medium

Wand, Shadow Word: Pain, Mind Blast, and Power Word: Shield keep downtime low once Spirit Tap arrives. Night Elf priests get two racial spells by quest: Starshards at 10 (a channelled Arcane nuke that does real damage while leveling) and Elune's Grace at 20 (a dodge and ranged-avoidance cooldown). Class quests are otherwise thin until Cenarion Aid at 50, so nothing pulls you off the zone route.

Warrior Slowest

Gear-dependent and downtime-heavy, and a Night Elf brings no weapon racial to help. Defensive Stance at 10 by killing Vorlus Vilehoof at the hidden moonwell south of Ban'ethil, then Elura's shipwreck in Darkshore for an Elunite weapon from Mathiel; Fire Hardened armor pieces at 28–30 (Mathiel's leggings are yours by default); Berserker Stance at 30 on Fray Island and the Whirlwind weapon chain at 35–40. Run Blackfathom Deeps and the Stockade for weapons, buy bandages by the stack.

Forever

Night Elves keep these five classes in Forever and gain Mage; the other new combinations go elsewhere (Undead paladin, Dwarf shaman, Gnome priest, Human hunter, Troll warlock, Orc mage). The Alliance Skyborne can be druids, so Night Elf stops being the only Alliance druid. Every talent tree is reworked, so the speed ranking above is Era's and may reshuffle. Fear Ward becomes baseline for every priest.

Rules of the road

Hearthstone policy

  • Bind it where the page tells you, and nowhere else. Every bracket has one hearth and one flight point to collect.
  • Darnassus has no flight master and no boat of its own: Rut'theran Village below it has both. Bind in the field hub you are questing from; hearth to Darnassus only for a class quest or a trainer visit, then take the portal down and fly out. From 25 on, Ironforge and Stormwind are usually closer than home.
  • Log out in an inn or a capital. Rested XP doubles kill experience; a night's log-off is worth a bar and a half early on.

Kill order

  • Kill everything green or yellow on the way to an objective. Red is downtime, grey is nothing.
  • Turn in quests in batches. Walk the hub once per loop, not once per quest.
  • Skip escorts that fail on a bad pull, group quests you cannot solo two levels late, and anything that sends you across an ocean for a single turn-in.

Night Elf-specific habits

  • Bind a key to Shadowmeld on day one. In Era it skips patrols and lets you log out in the field; in Forever it drops combat, and the Elune's Light crit burst goes on the same bar.
  • Learn First Aid in Dolanaar from Byancie verify at the hut west of the inn 55,57. Linen from the Gnarlpine furbolgs pays for it; bandages are your only emergency button until 20.
  • Skinning plus Herbalism from Dolanaar onward. Teldrassil and Darkshore are all nightsabers, bears and spiders, and the Kaldorei herbs (Peacebloom, Silverleaf, Earthroot) are the Cure Poison and Aquatic Form reagents you need anyway. Mining is poor here until Ashenvale.
  • Class quests are in the steps, shaded and tagged. Do them when they appear: each one is a spell, form or pet you cannot buy, and the level-10 ones (Defensive Stance, the pet, Blade of Cunning, Starshards, Bear Form) are the single biggest speed jump in the route. All five end in Darnassus, so do them on one trip.

Gold

  • Sell everything grey. Vendor whites that are not your weapon upgrade.
  • Mount money at 40 is 100 gold: 20 for Apprentice Riding from Jartsam and 80 for a Striped Nightsaber, Spotted Frostsaber or Striped Frostsaber from Lelanai, both in the Cenarion Enclave in Darnassus verify. Druids have Travel Form at 30, so they can push the cat to 45 if gold is short.
  • Buy weapons, not armor. A green weapon two levels above yours is the one purchase that pays back.

Before you move

Turn on Auto Loot in the Interface options. Bind a key to Shadowmeld. The class scroll you spawn with is a free turn-in at your trainer inside the tree. The glen is two loops: the grell camps and ponds south and west of the tree, and Shadowthread Cave to the north-west. Dying is a shortcut here: Wisp Spirit makes the run back faster than walking.

Leave at 5 with Crown of the Earth, Dolanaar Delivery and Zenn's Bidding in the log; kill what stands near the road so you hit 6 in town.

Forever

Nothing announced touches Shadowglen. Elune's Light should be active from level 1: the Grellkin camp is where you first feel a 10% crit racial. A Night Elf mage would start here in Forever; no trainer or level-10 quest is published.

Two ways to run it

Stranglethorn has the most quests of any zone but they spread from 30 to 45. Do the Shimmering Flats first (flat ground, no travel, 30–32), then Stranglethorn's 30–35 layer (Nesingwary's first tiers, the Rebel Camp, Booty Bay's coastal jobs), Hillsbrad at 34–35, and Desolace whenever the jungle gets thin. Buy food and water in Booty Bay before you leave it; the jungle sells nothing between camps.

Leave at 35. Fly Southshore, ride north over the Thandol Span into Arathi, or fly Menethil → Refuge Pointe once you have it. Refuge Pointe has no inn in Era: keep the hearth at Southshore.

Forever

Nothing announced touches Stranglethorn, Thousand Needles or Desolace for the Alliance. Shen'Dralas, the new elven-ruins zone, opens off the Valley of Bones in southern Desolace with no published level range; if it is 30–35 it replaces the Desolace swing and Nijel's Point is the door.

Darnassus logistics

Getting around the city

  • Cenarion Enclave (north-west): the druid trainers around Mathrengyl Bearwalker on the upper level 35,10 and Arch Druid Fandral Staghelm at the top of the tree 35,9; the hunter trainers and Jocaste and Dorion 40,9 verify; the rogue trainers, Syurna and Erion Shadewhisper, up the spiral ramp 32,16 verify; Rellian Greenspyre 38,22; Lelanai and Jartsam for the nightsaber and riding verify.
  • Warrior's Terrace (north-east): the warrior trainers and Elanaria 57,35, the weapon master Ilyenia Moonfire 57,47 verify.
  • Craftsmen's Terrace (east): the inn (Innkeeper Saelienne 64,17 verify), most profession trainers, First Aid. Tradesmen's Terrace (south-east): the auction house, the vendors, Smith Mathiel verify.
  • Temple of the Moon (south-west): the priest trainers Lariia and Astarii Starseeker, Priestess A'moora, Tyrande Whisperwind. Temple Gardens (centre): the bank in the great tree 41,44 verify, and the portal to Rut'theran Village on the west edge 29,41.
  • Darnassus has no flight master and no boat. Rut'theran Village below the portal has both: Vesprystus 58,94 (Teldrassil map) and the Moonspray dock to Auberdine. Every "go to Darnassus" in this guide means: fly or sail to Rut'theran, walk through the portal.

Boats and the Tram

  • Rut'theran ↔ Auberdine (the Moonspray) is the way home; the Auberdine hippogryph flies the same hop for free once you have Rut'theran's point.
  • Auberdine ↔ Menethil Harbor (the Bravery) is the road to the Eastern Kingdoms from 23 on. Menethil ↔ Theramore (the Lady Mehley) is the second crossing from 30. Booty Bay ↔ Ratchet (the Maiden's Fancy, neutral) links Stranglethorn to the Barrens. Stormwind Harbor does not exist in Era.
  • The Deeprun Tram runs Ironforge (Tinker Town) ↔ Stormwind (Dwarven District), free and about a minute. From 25 on those two cities are usually closer than home; both have every Night Elf class trainer.

Class quest stops

  • Level 4: priest In Favor of Elune, Shadowglen to Dolanaar.
  • Level 10: every class, all ending in Darnassus. Warrior to Elanaria on the Warrior's Terrace and Vorlus Vilehoof's moonwell; hunter to Dazalar in Dolanaar, then Jocaste in the Enclave; rogue to Syurna in the Enclave and Sethir at the Oracle Glade; priest to Lariia in the Temple for Starshards; druid to Mathrengyl Bearwalker, Moonglade, and the Moonkin Stone east of Auberdine.
  • Levels 12 and 14: warrior Shade of Elura at the Auberdine wreck, then Mathiel's Elunite weapon; druid Cure Poison through Bearwalker, Dendrite and Alanndarian Nightsong in Auberdine.
  • Level 16: druid Aquatic Form (Lake Elune'ara, the Darkshore and Westfall pendant halves); rogue Erion's Behest, then Lucius in Lakeshire.
  • Level 20: priest Elune's Grace from Astarii Starseeker; rogue Klaven's Tower on the Westfall border from Mathias Shaw in Stormwind.
  • Levels 28–30: warrior Fire Hardened armor from Mathiel, Klockmort Spannerspan and Grimand Elmore; warrior The Islander (Fray Island) then the Whirlwind chain from Bath'rah in Alterac.
  • Level 40: nothing by quest. Riding from Jartsam and the nightsaber from Lelanai in the Cenarion Enclave.
  • Level 50: every class has a Sunken Temple chain from any capital trainer: warrior Fallen Hero (Swamp of Sorrows), hunter and priest Ogtinc (Azshara), rogue Jorach Ravenholdt (Hillsbrad) then Xylem (Azshara), druid Torwa Pathfinder (Un'Goro).

Mount and gold

  • Apprentice Riding from Jartsam and the Striped Nightsaber, Spotted Frostsaber or Striped Frostsaber from Lelanai, both in the Cenarion Enclave in Darnassus verify. Together 100 gold at level 40 before reputation discounts. At 40 the route is in Dustwallow: fly Theramore → Auberdine, boat to Rut'theran, walk the portal, buy, and go back the same way.
  • Druids learn Travel Form at 30 from any trainer: 40% run speed outdoors, instant, no gold. It does not replace a cat at 60, but it lets a druid spend the 100 gold on weapons first and buy the mount at 43–45 when the money is easy. No Night Elf class gets a free mount.
  • Realistic gold by 40 on this route with Skinning plus Herbalism and nothing bought but weapons: 130–170. Without gathering: 70–90, which is why the 38–40 leg sits in Dustwallow and Stranglethorn, where the humanoids drop cloth and vendor greens.
  • Epic Swift Frostsaber, Swift Mistsaber or Swift Stormsaber at 60: 1,000 gold, 900 with Darnassus Exalted. Every Teldrassil, Darkshore and Ashenvale quest counts toward that rep, as do the Darnassus-tagged chains in Feralas and Winterspring.

WoW Forever watchlist

Confirmed as of Sep 19, 2026

World of Warcraft: Forever is Blizzard's permanent Classic+ ruleset: the original continents with new zones, dungeons, raids and quests, a talent rework, and a level cap that stays at 60. Beta runs Sep 17 to Oct 21, 2026 (level 20 cap, rising to 30); launch is Nov 4, 2026.
