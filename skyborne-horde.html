The Falling Sky

A hub-by-hub route for a Horde Skyborne: Zephras Isle to level 12, the airship down to Mulgore, then the Horde Kalimdor road to 60, with the class quests for Warrior, Hunter, Rogue, Druid and Shaman folded into the steps. Pick your class once and the page shows only yours. The Skyborne do not exist in Classic Era, so the whole page is beta data; the isle itself is tester reports, and everything below 12 that is not certain is flagged.

Start here

This is a route, not an encyclopedia. Each bracket tells you where to bind your hearthstone, which flight point to grab, and a numbered order of quests to run. Tick the boxes as you go; the page remembers them in this browser only.

Steps shaded in blue are class quests. Choose your class in Pick your class and the other four classes' steps disappear; the choice is remembered, and ?class=rogue in the address bar does the same thing. With no class chosen every class step shows, each with a tag.

Two kinds of callouts appear throughout. Era would be the Classic Era baseline; the Skyborne have none, so it only appears in the racials table to say so. Forever marks what WoW Forever has publicly changed or added and what to re-check in the beta client. Where a coordinate, count or quest name comes from a tester's forum post or from memory rather than a checked source it is tagged verify; treat those as "the right area", not gospel. On Zephras Isle that is almost everything: only two quest names are public and no coordinates are.

47,60 style numbers are map coordinates for the zone named in that step. Quest names are in bold. Coordinates were checked for the Barrens, Stonetalon, the Needles and the class chains; elsewhere they are close enough to find the NPC with the map open.

The route is the Tauren route with a different first chapter. A Windshaper Skyborne lands in Mulgore at 12, so from there the hearth, trainers and flight net are the same as a Tauren's: Thunder Bluff and the Crossroads, then Kalimdor as long as the quests hold out, with Eastern Kingdoms legs from 30 through the Orgrimmar zeppelin tower. Rogues are the exception: their trainers and class quests live in Orgrimmar's Cleft of Shadow, so a rogue binds there instead of Thunder Bluff whenever the two are equivalent.

Hunter Forever Fastest

A pet from level 10 and nothing ever touches you again. The isle has its own taming chain (three local winged beasts, which show as the Fox family once tamed verify), then Beast Training. Keep the pet fed, keep arrows stocked, and Walk on Air out of anything that goes wrong on a cliff.

Shaman Forever Fast / safest

Totems arrive by quest, not by trainer: Earth at 4 (the isle version is unreported verify), Fire at 10, Water at 20, Air at 30, all the standard Horde chains. Ghost Wolf at 20 is a 40% mount you already own, and Elemental Insight makes every manifestation fight shorter. The first elf shaman on the Horde side.

Druid Forever Medium, then fast

Slow until Bear Form at 10 (the Moonglade chain works for Skyborne per beta testers; Lunaclaw waits in the Barrens), steady until Cat Form at 20, then fast. Skyborne forms are sky-blue, feathered avian hybrids for Bear, Cat, Travel and Moonkin. Which druid trainer a Horde Skyborne reports to is unconfirmed; this page routes through Turak Runetotem in Thunder Bluff verify.

Rogue Forever Medium

Stealth past everything, kill one thing at a time. The class quests are Orgrimmar's Shattered Hand chain: Blade of Cunning at 10 (Therzok), Thistle Tea at 16–18 (Wrenix in Ratchet), Poisons at 20 (Shenthul), all reachable from the Barrens. Nothing on the isle is rogue-specific. Ravenholdt at 50 for the Sunken Temple mask.

Warrior Forever Slowest

Gear-dependent and downtime-heavy, and the Skyborne bring no health racial to soften it. Stances come by quest: Defensive at 10 (Veteran Uzzek in the Barrens), Berserker at 30 (Fray Island) alongside the Whirlwind weapon chain. Run Wailing Caverns and Razorfen Kraul for weapons, buy Heavy Linen Bandages by the stack.

Forever

All five combinations are new in Forever; there is no Era baseline for any of them. The speed ranking is the Era ranking of the same classes on other Horde races and every talent tree is reworked, so it may reshuffle. Beta testers report standard Classic class chains for hunters and druids and no Skyborne-only class quest for anyone verify.

Rules of the road

Hearthstone policy

  • Bind it where the page tells you, and nowhere else. Every bracket has one hearth and one flight point to collect.
  • Your hearth starts bound on Zephras Isle (Thendal Village, presumably) verify. The isle is a place you leave; the first thing to do off the airship is rebind on the mainland, in Thunder Bluff, then at the Crossroads once you are questing there.
  • Thunder Bluff is a dead end on the flight map: nothing zeppelins from it. Bind there only while Mulgore and the Barrens are the route; from 25 on bind at the hub you are questing from. Rogues can bind in Orgrimmar from 12 and lose nothing.
  • Log out in an inn or a capital. Rested XP doubles kill experience; a night's log-off is worth a bar and a half early on.

Kill order

  • Kill everything green or yellow on the way to an objective. Red is downtime, grey is nothing.
  • Turn in quests in batches. Walk the hub once per loop, not once per quest.
  • Skip escorts that fail on a bad pull, group quests you cannot solo two levels late, and anything that sends you across an ocean for a single turn-in.

Skyborne-specific habits

  • Bind Walk on Air and Skysight on day one. Skysight every two minutes while moving; Walk on Air off every cliff instead of finding the path down.
  • Learn First Aid in Thendal or Valanaar if a trainer exists verify; otherwise on the ground floor of Thunder Bluff's main rise at 12. Bandages are your only emergency button until 20.
  • Skinning plus Herbalism from the isle onward, or Mining if you are a warrior who wants Brutal Armor's Iron Bars. There is no Cultivation for you, so Herbalism is a normal gathering pick.
  • Class quests are in the steps, shaded and tagged. Do them when they appear: each one is a spell or form you cannot buy, and the level-10 ones (stance, pet, Blade of Cunning, Fire totem, Bear Form) are the single biggest speed jump in the route.
  • You are a guest of the Horde, not a member, in the dialogue; nothing mechanical is reported to follow from that, but Thunder Bluff rep starts at neutral verify.

Gold

  • Sell everything grey. Vendor whites that are not your weapon upgrade.
  • Mount money at 40 is 100 gold: 20 for Apprentice Riding and 80 for the mount. The Skyborne mount is the Galestrider, datamined as a reputation reward with no vendor found yet verify; the fallback is a kodo from Harb Clawhoof in Bloodhoof Village at Thunder Bluff Exalted, or a wolf, raptor or skeletal horse at the matching city's Exalted. Shamans have Ghost Wolf at 20 and druids Travel Form at 30, so both can push the purchase to 45.
  • Buy weapons, not armor. A green weapon two levels above yours is the one purchase that pays back.

Read first

No quest list for Zephras Isle has been published. Blizzard puts every zone at 50 to 200 quests; testers finish the isle at 13 to 14. What follows is the story order from beta reports, the two quest names that are public, and the subzone names from the beta map, split across the four settlements at levels that are an estimate verify. Take every quest a hub offers and clear it before moving on; the isle is linear and you will not miss anything by doing so.

Before you move

Turn on Auto Loot in the Interface options. Bind keys to Walk on Air and Skysight. Read the class scroll you spawn with if there is one: it is a free turn-in at your trainer verify. The zone plays as a U: start here in the north-west, south and downhill through forest and farmland, then back up to the north-east and the finale.

Leave at 5, south into the Shen'dar Highlands. There is no breadcrumb between the villages; testers complained about it.

Forever

Blessing of Zephras (+40% run speed for 5 minutes, breaks on hostile action) is datamined for this zone; its source object is undocumented verify. Skysight gets its 15-minute duration at special isle locations, so cast it near anything that looks like an elemental convergence. Access to the isle requires the Skyborne Heroic Pack or higher.

Off the airship

You land on the northern cliffs of Mulgore verify. Go to Thunder Bluff first: bind at Innkeeper Pala, get the flight point from Tal, train, learn First Aid if the isle had no trainer, break in the professions. Archdruid Hamuul on Elder Rise gives The Barrens Oases and Melor Stonehoof on Hunter Rise The Hunter's Way, both Crossroads deliveries to carry along. Then out through the Great Gate 71,61 on the east road; Camp Taurajo is the first thing you see. Grab its flight point, Kirge Sternhorn's Journey to the Crossroads, and a look at Ruga Ragetotem if you are a warrior, then the road north. On a PvP realm the Crossroads is the most contested town in the game; quest out of Camp Taurajo whenever it is on fire.

Leave at 20. Fly Camp Taurajo → Sun Rock Retreat and rebind there.

Forever

The 15–25 Undercity-surface zone is the Forsaken's answer to a crowded Barrens; a Skyborne only goes if the Crossroads is a war zone. Shen'Dralas, the new elven-ruins zone, sits between Mulgore and Desolace with no published level range, and its Highborne story is your own people's; if it is 15–25 it slots in here instead, and the Mulgore plateau you landed on has a path into Desolace.

Two ways to run it

Stranglethorn has the most quests of any zone but they spread from 30 to 45. Do the 30–35 layer now (Nesingwary's first two tiers, Grom'gol's troll war, Booty Bay's coastal jobs), go to Desolace for 32–35, and return to Stranglethorn at 37 and again at 42 as the higher tiers open. Buy food and water in Grom'gol before you leave it; the jungle sells nothing between camps.

Leave at 35. Fly Shadowprey → Orgrimmar, zeppelin to Undercity, fly to Tarren Mill, road east into Arathi. Rebind at Hammerfall. This is the one leg that runs through Undercity: Hammerfall has no flight point until you have walked to it.

Forever

Shen'Dralas opens off the Valley of Bones in southern Desolace. If its level range turns out to be 30–35 it replaces the Desolace swing outright, and it is the Highborne ruins your ancestors fled: expect story hooks for a Skyborne verify. Rebuilt Dalaran in Alterac is also a 30ish hub with its own quests and the 28–33 City of Dalaran dungeon; Bath'rah is a short ride from it.

Thunder Bluff and Orgrimmar logistics

Getting around Thunder Bluff

  • Three elevators reach the mesa from the ground: the main one on the south-west side by the road from Bloodhoof, and two on the east. Walk on Air makes the rim a shortcut rather than a death.
  • Central rise (where the elevators land): bank, auction house, flight master Tal on the west edge 47,49, the inn (Innkeeper Pala, at the bottom of the rise near the bank). Bind here at 12.
  • Hunter Rise (south): warrior and hunter trainers (Torm Ragetotem and Holt Thunderhorn give the class quests), Cairne Bloodhoof.
  • Elder Rise (east): druid trainers around Archdruid Hamuul Runetotem; Turak Runetotem for the druid chains verify; Rahauro's Ragefire quests, Auld Stonespire's Razorfen quest.
  • Spirit Rise (north): shaman trainers and Xanis Flameweaver for the totem chains.
  • Professions cluster on the central rise's lower ring and the bridges; First Aid and cooking are on the ground level of the main rise verify. No rogue trainer anywhere in the city.

Orgrimmar for rogues

  • The Cleft of Shadow (the cave in the centre of the city) holds the rogue trainers, Therzok, Zando'zan and Shenthul. The inn is in the Valley of Strength by the front gate; the flight master is by the zeppelin gate.
  • Orgrimmar's zeppelin tower has platforms for Undercity and Grom'gol; Thunder Bluff has none, so every Eastern Kingdoms leg starts with a flight here.
  • Ratchet ↔ Booty Bay boat links the Barrens to Stranglethorn without a zeppelin.

Mount and gold

  • The Skyborne racial mount family is the Galestrider: Empyrean, Regal, Stormy and Umber at the level-40 60% tier, Swift versions at 60. They are listed as reputation rewards in the beta data, presumably Windshaper reputation for the Horde variant; no vendor, price or reputation rank has been found verify. Riding is TBC-style: Apprentice at 40, epic at 60.
  • If the Galestrider is not buyable at 40, the Classic rule applies: any Horde race's mount at Exalted with that race's city. Thunder Bluff is the reputation this route feeds (every Mulgore, Barrens, Feralas and Desolace Tauren quest), so Harb Clawhoof's kodos in Bloodhoof Village are the fallback, with Kar Stormsinger next to him for riding. 100 gold total.
  • Shamans have Ghost Wolf at 20 (40% speed, usable indoors, instant with the talent) and druids Travel Form at 30. Neither replaces a mount at 60, but both let you spend the 100 gold on weapons first and buy the mount at 43–45 when the money is easy.
  • The Cerulean Prideclaw, the ground mount in the Skyborne packs, is usable by all races; whether it needs riding skill at 40 or is a free mount is unreported verify.
  • Realistic gold by 40 on this route with Herbalism plus Skinning and nothing bought but weapons: 130–170. Without gathering: 70–90, which is why the 38–40 bracket sits in Dustwallow, where the dragonkin drop vendor greens.
  • Epic mount at 60: 1,000 gold, 900 at Exalted with whichever reputation sells it.

WoW Forever watchlist

Confirmed as of Sep 19, 2026

World of Warcraft: Forever is Blizzard's permanent Classic+ ruleset: the original continents with new zones, dungeons, raids and quests, a talent rework, and a level cap that stays at 60. Beta runs Sep 17 to Oct 21, 2026 (level 20 cap, rising to 30); launch is Nov 4, 2026. For a Skyborne everything on this page is Forever, so the table below lists what is most likely to move.

Sources and verification

Zephras Isle content comes from Blizzard's panel recaps, the Wowhead Forever database (zone 16593, races 95 and 96, factions 2778 and 2779, the racial spell tooltips), Warcraft Wiki's subzone list from the beta map, Blizzard Watch's hands-on, and beta tester reports on the MMO-Champion megathread and the Blizzard forums (Eldryth's end-to-end Horde account is the only one). Nothing on the isle has coordinates or a verified quest order. From 12 on the route is the Tauren guide's: Barrens, Stonetalon, Ashenvale and Needles quest objectives were checked against wow-pro's Barrens 12–20, Jame's Horde Chapter I, Wowhead Classic and Warcraft Wiki; the 30–60 brackets are hub-and-chain level from Classic knowledge and shared with the Undead and Tauren guides. Class quests were checked quest by quest against Warcraft Wiki, Wowhead Classic and Icy Veins; the rogue chains are the Orc/Troll ones and the assumption that a Skyborne gets them is tagged verify wherever it matters.
