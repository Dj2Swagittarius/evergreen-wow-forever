The Deep Road

A hub-by-hub Dwarf leveling route from Coldridge Valley to level 60, with the class quests for Warrior, Paladin, Hunter, Rogue and Priest folded into the steps, and the Forever-only Dwarf Shaman marked where it will slot in. Pick your class once and the page shows only yours. Built on Classic Era rules; everything WoW Forever is known to change is flagged in violet.

Start here

This is a route, not an encyclopedia. Each bracket tells you where to bind your hearthstone, which flight point to grab, and a numbered order of quests to run. Tick the boxes as you go; the page remembers them in this browser only.

Steps shaded in bronze are class quests. Choose your class in Pick your class and the other classes' steps disappear; the choice is remembered, and ?class=hunter in the address bar does the same thing. With no class chosen every class step shows, each with a tag. Shaman is a Forever-only Dwarf class: its button is there, but its quests are unpublished and only a placeholder is listed.

Two kinds of callouts appear throughout. Era is the verified Classic Era baseline. Forever marks what WoW Forever has publicly changed or added, and what to re-check once the beta is playable. Where a coordinate or count comes from memory rather than a checked source it is tagged verify; treat those as "the right area", not gospel.

47,53 style numbers are map coordinates for the zone named in that step. Quest names are in bold. Coordinates were checked for Dun Morogh, Loch Modan and the class chains; elsewhere they are close enough to find the NPC with the map open.

The route stays in the Eastern Kingdoms as long as the quests hold out, because a Dwarf's hearth, trainers and flight net all sit between Ironforge and Menethil Harbor, one flight apart. The Deeprun Tram makes Stormwind a free extension of Ironforge for the Cathedral, SI:7 and the Dwarven District errands. Kalimdor legs start at 23 (a boat from Menethil to Auberdine for Ashenvale) and again at 30 (Thousand Needles by way of Theramore).

Solo leveling speed, 1–60

Showing every class quest. Pick one and the other classes' steps are hidden everywhere on the page; the Evergreen addon does the same in-game from your character's class.

Hunter Fastest

A pet at 10 from Grif Wildheart's three tames in Kharanos, a gun with the racial +5 skill, and from then on the pet tanks while you shoot and never sit. Training the Beast at Belia Thundergranite in the Hall of Arms, then nothing pulls you off the zone route until Ogtinc's Sunken Temple chain at 50 for the Devilsaur Eye. Starving Winter Wolves at Iceflow Lake are the classic first pet.

Rogue Fast

Ambush, Sinister Strike and Stoneform against every poisoner and bleeder. Blade of Cunning at 10 from Hulfdan Blackbeard in the Forlorn Cavern by way of Onin MacHammar at Gnomeregan, lockpicking practice at 16 at Alther's Mill, Poisons at 20 through Klaven's Tower on the Westfall border, Ravenholdt at 24 and again at 50. Bandages and Evasion cover the pulls that go wrong.

Paladin Medium / safest

Never dies, never hurries. Redemption by quest at 12 through Tiza and Muiredon Battleforge in the Hall of Mysteries, Sense Undead and the Bastion of Stormwind at 20, then Verigan's Fist at 22 from Jordan Stilwell at Ironforge's own gate, the best two-hander a leveling paladin will hold until 40, when the Warhorse is free. Seal of the Crusader plus Judgement, a two-handed mace for the Forever racial, and Consecration when it comes.

Priest Medium

Wand, Shadow Word: Pain, Mind Blast and Power Word: Shield keep downtime low once Spirit Tap arrives. Dwarf priests get Desperate Prayer at 10 from Maxan Anvol and Fear Ward at 20 from Toldren Deepiron, both talk-and-learn quests; Fear Ward is the reason raids want a Dwarf priest. Class quests are otherwise thin until Cenarion Aid at 50, so nothing pulls you off the zone route.

Warrior Slowest

Gear-dependent and downtime-heavy, but Stoneform is the warrior's missing defensive cooldown. Defensive Stance at 10 by bringing Vejrek's head to Muren Stormpike in the Hall of Arms, then Tormus Deepforge's Grey Iron weapon from Ironband's Compound; Fire Hardened armor at 28–30 with Klockmort in Tinker Town nearest; Berserker Stance at 30 on Fray Island and the Whirlwind weapon chain at 35–40. Run Gnomeregan and the Stockade for weapons, buy bandages by the stack.

Shaman Forever Unpublished

New Dwarf and class combination in WoW Forever. As of Sep 18, 2026 Blizzard has published no Dwarf shaman story, starting quest, clan or totem quests; Dwarves start in Coldridge Valley on the ordinary path. Whether Call of Earth, Fire, Water and Air get Alliance versions or the totems are trainer-taught is unknown. Only the level-50 Sunken Temple chain is Classic and faction-neutral. Forever

Dwarves keep these five classes in Forever and gain Shaman; the other new combinations go elsewhere (Undead paladin, Human hunter, Gnome priest, Night Elf mage, Troll warlock, Orc mage). Every talent tree is reworked, so the speed ranking above is Era's and may reshuffle. Fear Ward, the Dwarf priest's racial spell, becomes baseline for every priest, so the level-20 quest may vanish or change.

Rules of the road

Hearthstone policy

  • Bind it where the page tells you, and nowhere else. Every bracket has one hearth and one flight point to collect.
  • Ironforge is the northern hub of the Alliance flight net and the Deeprun Tram makes Stormwind part of it. Bind in the field hub you are questing from; hearth to the capital only when a class quest or a trainer visit is due, then fly back out.
  • Log out in an inn or a capital. Rested XP doubles kill experience; a night's log-off is worth a bar and a half early on.

Kill order

  • Kill everything green or yellow on the way to an objective. Red is downtime, grey is nothing.
  • Turn in quests in batches. Walk the hub once per loop, not once per quest.
  • Skip escorts that fail on a bad pull, group quests you cannot solo two levels late, and anything that sends you across an ocean for a single turn-in.

Dwarf-specific habits

  • Bind Stoneform next to your healing potion. It is a dispel and a shield in one press: use it the moment a troll or spider poisons you, or when two extra mobs arrive, not after the pull has gone wrong.
  • Hunters buy guns and nothing else in Era; the +5 skill is the racial, and Hegnar Rumbleshot in Dun Morogh and Timberline Arms in Ironforge keep you in ammo. Warriors and paladins buy maces if Forever is the target, swords or axes otherwise.
  • Learn First Aid in Kharanos from the physician in the Thunderbrew Distillery (Thamner Pol verify). Wolf and trogg linen pays for it; bandages are your only emergency button besides Stoneform until 20.
  • Mining plus Skinning from Kharanos onward. Dun Morogh, Loch Modan and the Wetlands are copper and tin country, and Skinning is gold with no detours from a starting zone full of boars, bears and wolves.
  • Class quests are in the steps, shaded and tagged. Do them when they appear: each one is a spell, pet or weapon you cannot buy, and the level-10 ones (Defensive Stance, the three tames, Blade of Cunning, Desperate Prayer, Redemption at 12) are the single biggest speed jump in the route.

Gold

  • Sell everything grey. Vendor whites that are not your weapon upgrade.
  • Mount money at 40 is 100 gold: 20 for Apprentice Riding from Ultham Ironhorn and 80 for a Brown, Gray or White Ram from Veron Amberstill, both at Amberstill Ranch in eastern Dun Morogh. Paladins get the Warhorse free at 40 and spend the 100 gold on weapons.
  • Buy weapons, not armor. A green weapon two levels above yours is the one purchase that pays back.

Two ways to run it

Stranglethorn has the most quests of any zone but they spread from 30 to 45. Do the Shimmering Flats first (flat ground, no travel, 30–32), then Stranglethorn's 30–35 layer (Nesingwary's first tiers, the Rebel Camp, Booty Bay's coastal jobs), Hillsbrad at 34–35, and Desolace whenever the jungle gets thin. Buy food and water in Booty Bay before you leave it; the jungle sells nothing between camps. Big Game Hunter in Forever makes the Nesingwary tiers the best kills in the game for a Dwarf.

Leave at 35. Fly Southshore, ride north over the Thandol Span into Arathi, or fly Menethil → Refuge Pointe once you have it. Refuge Pointe has no inn in Era: keep the hearth at Southshore.

Forever

Nothing announced touches Stranglethorn, Thousand Needles or Desolace for the Alliance. Shen'Dralas, the new elven-ruins zone, opens off the Valley of Bones in southern Desolace with no published level range; if it is 30–35 it replaces the Desolace swing and Nijel's Point is the door.

Ironforge logistics

Getting around the city

  • The Commons (inside the gate): the bank and the auction house 25,74 verify, the Stonefire Tavern (Innkeeper Firebrew), John Turner for the paladin linen errand, the general goods and profession vendors.
  • The Great Forge (centre): Gryth Thurden the gryphon master 55,48, Tormus Deepforge for the warrior's Grey Iron weapon 49,43 verify, the Ironforge Physician for First Aid 55,58, the Deep Mountain Mining Guild 52,26 verify, most profession trainers.
  • Military Ward (south-east): the Hall of Arms holds the warrior trainers 66,89 with Muren Stormpike 71,90 verify, and the hunter trainers 71,84 with Olmin Burningbeard and the pet trainer Belia Thundergranite 71,86 by the cannon; Timberline Arms weapon masters 62,89.
  • Mystic Ward (north-west): the Hall of Mysteries holds the paladin trainers (Tiza and Muiredon Battleforge 24,5 verify) and the priest trainers (High Priest Rohan, Toldren Deepiron 26,9); the wand vendor 23,16; Talvash del Kissel for the Uldaman necklace.
  • Forlorn Cavern (north): the rogue trainers with Hulfdan Blackbeard 52,15, the fishing trainer, and the warlock hall that a Dwarf never needs.
  • Hall of Explorers (north-east): Prospector Stormpike, Librarian Mae Paledust, Roetten Stonehammer, Historian Karnik, Advisor Belgrum and Krom Stoutarm: the Loch Modan, Desolace, Uldaman and Azshara hooks all start here.
  • Tinker Town (east): the Deeprun Tram 76,51, Gnoarn (Find Bingles), Klockmort Spannerspan (Fire Hardened Gauntlets), Tinkmaster Overspark and the Gnomeregan quests.
  • The High Seat 39,58: King Magni Bronzebeard, Senator Barin Redstone for The Reports verify, and Kharan Mighthammer's step of the Onyxia chain.

Tram and boats

  • The Deeprun Tram runs Tinker Town ↔ Dwarven District, Stormwind, free and about a minute. Every "go to Stormwind" in this guide means the Tram: the Cathedral for Duthorian Rall and Lord Grayson Shadowbreaker, SI:7 for Mathias Shaw, Brohann Caskbelly and Furen Longbeard in the Dwarven District, the Stockade.
  • Menethil Harbor (Wetlands) is the Alliance port: the Bravery to Auberdine (Darkshore) and the Lady Mehley to Theramore (Dustwallow). Ironforge → Menethil is one flight once you have it; Thelsamar is the stepping stone before that.
  • Auberdine ↔ Rut'theran (the Moonspray) reaches Darnassus; Booty Bay ↔ Ratchet (the Maiden's Fancy, neutral) links Stranglethorn to the Barrens without a boat to Kalimdor's north. Stormwind Harbor does not exist in Era.

Mount and gold

  • Apprentice Riding from Ultham Ironhorn and the Brown, Gray or White Ram from Veron Amberstill, both at Amberstill Ranch on the road east of Kharanos 63,50 verify, not in Ironforge. Together 100 gold at level 40 before reputation discounts. A Dwarf has no reputation racial, so the Ironforge discount comes from Dun Morogh, Loch Modan, Wetlands and Badlands quests alone.
  • Paladins learn Summon Warhorse from Duthorian Rall at 40, free; only the 20-gold riding lesson is left to pay, and the horse money goes on weapons.
  • Realistic gold by 40 on this route with Mining plus Skinning and nothing bought but weapons: 130–170. Copper, tin and iron from Dun Morogh, Loch Modan, the Wetlands and Arathi sell steadily on the auction house. Without gathering: 70–90, which is why the 38–40 leg sits in Dustwallow and Stranglethorn, where the humanoids drop cloth and vendor greens.
  • Epic Swift Brown, Gray or White Ram at 60: 1,000 gold, 900 with Ironforge Exalted. Every Dun Morogh, Loch Modan and Wetlands quest counts toward that rep, as do the Hall of Explorers chains through Uldaman and the Badlands.
