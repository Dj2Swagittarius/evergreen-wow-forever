The Long Walk

A hub-by-hub Tauren leveling route from Red Cloud Mesa to level 60, with the class quests for Warrior, Hunter, Shaman and Druid folded into the steps. Pick your class once and the page shows only yours. Built on Classic Era rules; everything WoW Forever is known to change is flagged in violet.

Start here

This is a route, not an encyclopedia. Each bracket tells you where to bind your hearthstone, which flight point to grab, and a numbered order of quests to run. Tick the boxes as you go; the page remembers them in this browser only.

Steps shaded in rust are class quests. Choose your class in Pick your class and the other three classes' steps disappear; the choice is remembered, and ?class=druid in the address bar does the same thing. With no class chosen every class step shows, each with a tag.

Two kinds of callouts appear throughout. Era is the verified Classic Era baseline. Forever marks what WoW Forever has publicly changed or added, and what to re-check once the beta is playable. Where a coordinate or count comes from memory rather than a checked source it is tagged verify; treat those as "the right area", not gospel.

47,60 style numbers are map coordinates for the zone named in that step. Quest names are in bold. Coordinates were checked for Mulgore, the Barrens and the class chains; elsewhere they are close enough to find the NPC with the map open.

The route stays on Kalimdor as long as the quests hold out, because a Tauren's hearth, trainers and flight net all sit between Thunder Bluff and the Crossroads. Eastern Kingdoms legs start at 30 (Stranglethorn) and are reached through the Orgrimmar zeppelin tower, never through Undercity unless a flight point is the reason.

Pick your class

Solo leveling speed, 1–60
Show quests for

Showing every class quest. Pick one and the other three classes' steps are hidden everywhere on the page; the Evergreen addon does the same in-game from your character's class.

Hunter Fastest

A pet from level 10 and nothing ever touches you again. The Tauren taming chain sends you at three Mulgore beasts (a Plainstrider, a Prairie Stalker and a Swoop) and then to Thunder Bluff for Tame Beast. Keep the pet fed, keep arrows stocked, and let Endurance cover the one time a mob reaches you.

Shaman Fast / safest

Totems arrive by quest, not by trainer: Earth at 4, Fire at 10, Water at 20, Air at 30. Ghost Wolf at 20 is a 40% mount you already own, Windfury at 30 turns a two-hander into the best leveling weapon in the game, and Healing Wave means you never sit to eat. The only Horde shaman race in Era with 5% more health.

Druid Medium, then fast

Slow until Bear Form at 10 (a Moonglade trip, then Lunaclaw in Mulgore), steady until Cat Form at 20, then fast: Cat kills, Bear tanks the extra pull, Aquatic Form at 16 swims the water quests, Travel Form at 30 plus Plainsrunning in Forever is a mount at 30. Tauren is the only Horde druid in Era; Forever adds Skyborne.

Warrior Slowest

Gear-dependent and downtime-heavy, but Endurance and War Stomp are the two racials a warrior wants most. Stances come by quest: Defensive at 10 (Veteran Uzzek in Durotar), Berserker at 30 (Fray Island in the Barrens) alongside the Whirlwind weapon chain. Run Wailing Caverns and Razorfen Kraul for weapons, buy Heavy Linen Bandages by the stack.

Forever

Tauren keep exactly these four classes in Forever; the new combinations go to other races (Undead paladin, Dwarf shaman, Troll warlock, Orc mage, Human hunter, Gnome priest). Every talent tree is reworked, so the speed ranking above is Era's and may reshuffle.

Rules of the road

Hearthstone policy

  • Bind it where the page tells you, and nowhere else. Every bracket has one hearth and one flight point to collect.
  • Thunder Bluff is a dead end on the flight map: nothing zeppelins from it. Bind there only while Mulgore and the Barrens are the route; from 25 on bind at the hub you are questing from.
  • Log out in an inn or a capital. Rested XP doubles kill experience; a night's log-off is worth a bar and a half early on.

Kill order

  • Kill everything green or yellow on the way to an objective. Red is downtime, grey is nothing.
  • Turn in quests in batches. Walk the hub once per loop, not once per quest.
  • Skip escorts that fail on a bad pull, group quests you cannot solo two levels late, and anything that sends you across an ocean for a single turn-in.

Tauren-specific habits

  • Bind a key to War Stomp on day one. Use it to interrupt a heal, to break a runner's cast, or to get four seconds of distance when a second mob joins.
  • Learn First Aid in Bloodhoof Village (Vira Younghoof). Linen from the Venture Co. and Bael'dun dwarves pays for it; bandages are your only emergency button until 20.
  • Herbalism plus Skinning from Bloodhoof onward. Cultivation makes Herbalism the obvious gathering pick; in Forever it hands you herbs you never bent down for.
  • Class quests are in the steps, shaded and tagged. Do them when they appear: each one is a spell or form you cannot buy, and the level-10 ones (stance, pet, Fire totem, Bear Form) are the single biggest speed jump in the route.

Gold

  • Sell everything grey. Vendor whites that are not your weapon upgrade.
  • Mount money at 40 is 100 gold: 20 for Apprentice Riding from Kar Stormsinger and 80 for a Brown or Gray Kodo from Harb Clawhoof, both in Bloodhoof Village. Shamans have Ghost Wolf at 20 and druids Travel Form at 30, so both can push the kodo to 45 if gold is short.
  • Buy weapons, not armor. A green weapon two levels above yours is the one purchase that pays back.
1–6

Red Cloud Mesa

HubCamp Narache 45,77 (Mulgore, south-west mesa)
HearthAlready bound to Camp Narache. No inn; nothing to do.
Flight pointNone. Mulgore has no flight master outside Thunder Bluff in Era.
TrainersAll four class trainers stand around the camp: Krang Stonehoof, Lanka Farshot, Seer Ravenfeather, Gart Mistrunner
Before you move

Turn on Auto Loot in the Interface options. Bind a key to War Stomp. Read the class scroll you spawn with: it is a free turn-in at your trainer. The mesa is one loop: camp, the well to the south-east, the cougar hills south, the quilboar ravine east, back to camp.

Leave at 6 with both deliveries in the log. North on the road out of the mesa; it drops straight into Bloodhoof Village.

Forever

Nothing announced touches Red Cloud Mesa. Plainsrunning should be active from level 1: the walk from the cougars to the ravine is where you first feel it.

Pickup sweep

One lap of the village: Baine Bloodhoof in the big tent 48,60 (turn in Rites, take Sharing the Land, Rite of Vision, Dwarven Digging at 8), Mull Thunderhorn 49,60 (Poison Water), Harken Windtotem 49,59 (Swoop Hunting), Ruul Eagletalon 47,62 (Dangers of the Windfury), Maur Raincaller 47,57 (Mazzranache), and Morin Cloudstalker on the road east of the village 57,60 (The Ravaged Caravan). Forever

Thunder Bluff's Forever zone page shows no notes, and no source reports Mulgore changes beyond new lighting and Mount Hyjal visible on the horizon from the north edge. Shen'Dralas, the new elven-ruins zone, opens off Desolace, not Mulgore, so this bracket should be untouched.

First stop

Camp Taurajo before anything else: the flight point, Kirge Sternhorn's Journey to the Crossroads, and a look at Ruga Ragetotem if you are a warrior. Then the road north to the Crossroads. On a PvP realm the Crossroads is the most contested town in the game; quest out of Camp Taurajo whenever it is on fire.

Leave at 20. Fly Camp Taurajo → Sun Rock Retreat and rebind there.

Forever

The 15–25 Undercity-surface zone is the Forsaken's answer to a crowded Barrens; a Tauren only goes if the Crossroads is a war zone. Shen'Dralas is confirmed to sit between Mulgore and Desolace with no published level range; if it turns out to be 15–25 it would slot in here instead.

Two ways to run it

Stranglethorn has the most quests of any zone but they spread from 30 to 45. Do the 30–35 layer now (Nesingwary's first two tiers, Grom'gol's troll war, Booty Bay's coastal jobs), go to Desolace for 32–35, and return to Stranglethorn at 37 and again at 42 as the higher tiers open. Buy food and water in Grom'gol before you leave it; the jungle sells nothing between camps.

Leave at 35. Fly Shadowprey → Orgrimmar, zeppelin to Undercity, fly to Tarren Mill, road east into Arathi. Rebind at Hammerfall. This is the one leg that runs through Undercity: Hammerfall has no flight point until you have walked to it.

Thunder Bluff logistics

Getting around the city

  • Three elevators reach the mesa from the ground: the main one on the south-west side by the road from Bloodhoof, and two on the east. Falling off the rim is fatal; falling off a bridge is only embarrassing.
  • Central rise (where the elevators land): bank, auction house, flight master Tal on the west edge 47,49, the inn (Innkeeper Pala, at the bottom of the rise near the bank).
  • Hunter Rise (south): warrior and hunter trainers, Cairne Bloodhoof, the Great Kodo vendors are not here (they are in Bloodhoof Village).
  • Elder Rise (east): druid trainers around Archdruid Hamuul Runetotem, Rahauro's Ragefire quests, Auld Stonespire's Razorfen quest.
  • Spirit Rise (north): shaman trainers, and the Pools of Vision beneath it hold the priest and mage trainers you do not need.
  • Professions cluster on the central rise's lower ring and the bridges; First Aid and cooking are on the ground level of the main rise verify.

Zeppelins and boats

  • Thunder Bluff has no zeppelin. Fly to Orgrimmar (the flight master is by the zeppelin gate) and walk out to the tower: platforms for Undercity and Grom'gol.
  • Ratchet ↔ Booty Bay boat links the Barrens to Stranglethorn without a zeppelin.
  • Undercity's tower returns to Orgrimmar and Grom'gol; you use it for Kargath, Stonard, Revantusk and the Plaguelands flights only.

Class quest stops

  • Level 4: shaman Call of Earth at Camp Narache (Seer Ravenfeather).
  • Level 10: every class. Warrior to Uzzek at Far Watch Post; hunter to Yaw Sharpmane in Bloodhoof, then Holt Thunderhorn on Hunter Rise; shaman Call of Fire from Xanis Flameweaver on Spirit Rise; druid Heeding the Call from Gennia Runetotem in Bloodhoof, then Turak Runetotem on Elder Rise and the Moonglade teleport.
  • Levels 14 and 16: druid Cure Poison and Aquatic Form, both through Turak and Moonglade.
  • Level 20: shaman Call of Water (Xanis → Islen Waterseer south of Ratchet); warrior Speak with Ruga at Camp Taurajo.
  • Level 30: shaman Call of Air (Prate Cloudseer, Thousand Needles); warrior The Islander (Fray Island) then the Whirlwind chain from Bath'rah in Alterac.
  • Level 50: every class has a Sunken Temple chain from its Thunder Bluff trainer: warrior Fallen Hero (Swamp of Sorrows), hunter Ogtinc (Azshara), shaman Bath'rah (Alterac), druid Torwa Pathfinder (Un'Goro).

Mount and gold

  • Apprentice Riding from Kar Stormsinger and the Brown, Gray or Great-coloured Kodo from Harb Clawhoof, both in Bloodhoof Village, not Thunder Bluff. Together 100 gold at level 40 before reputation discounts.
  • Shamans have Ghost Wolf at 20 (40% speed, usable indoors, instant with the talent) and druids Travel Form at 30. Neither replaces a kodo at 60, but both let you spend the 100 gold on weapons first and buy the kodo at 43–45 when the money is easy.
  • Realistic gold by 40 on this route with Herbalism plus Skinning and nothing bought but weapons: 130–170. Without gathering: 70–90, which is why the 38–40 bracket sits in Dustwallow, where the dragonkin drop vendor greens.
  • Epic Great Kodo at 60: 1,000 gold, 900 with Thunder Bluff Exalted. Every Mulgore and Barrens quest counts toward that rep, as do the Feralas and Desolace Tauren camps.
