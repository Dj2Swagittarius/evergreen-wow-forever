Start here

This is a route, not an encyclopedia. Each bracket tells you where to bind your hearthstone, which flight point to grab, and a numbered order of quests to run. Tick the boxes as you go; the page remembers them in this browser only.

Steps shaded in teal are class quests. Choose your class in Pick your class and the other classes' steps disappear; the choice is remembered, and ?class=priest in the address bar does the same thing. With no class chosen every class step shows, each with a tag. Warlock steps carry a violet tag as well: Troll warlocks exist only in WoW Forever, and their steps are the Orc chain until the beta says otherwise.

Two kinds of callouts appear throughout. Era is the verified Classic Era baseline. Forever marks what WoW Forever has publicly changed or added, and what to re-check once the beta is playable. Where a coordinate or count comes from memory rather than a checked source it is tagged verify; treat those as "the right area", not gospel.

47,60 style numbers are map coordinates for the zone named in that step. Quest names are in bold. Coordinates were checked for Durotar, the Barrens and the class chains; elsewhere they are close enough to find the NPC with the map open.

The route stays on Kalimdor as long as the quests hold out, because a Troll's hearth, trainers and flight net all sit between Orgrimmar and the Crossroads. Eastern Kingdoms legs start at 30 (Stranglethorn) and are reached through the zeppelin tower outside Orgrimmar's main gate, a two-minute walk from the Valley of Strength inn; Undercity is only ever a transfer.

Pick your class

Solo leveling speed, 1–60
Show quests for

Showing every class quest. Pick one and the other classes' steps are hidden everywhere on the page; the Evergreen addon does the same in-game from your character's class.

Hunter Fastest

A pet from level 10 and nothing ever touches you again. Thotar in Razor Hill sends you at a Dire Mottled Boar, a Surf Crawler and an Armored Scorpid, then to Ormak Grimshot in Orgrimmar for Beast Training. Bow Specialization makes the bow the obvious Era weapon; Beast Slaying covers the zones you level in. Keep the pet fed, keep arrows stocked.

Mage Fast

Frostbolt and Frost Nova kill everything before it reaches you, and you never buy food or water. Ju-Ju Heaps at 10 on the Echo Isles is the only early class quest; the Nether-lace robe at 26 and Tabetha's wand chain at 30–40 are worth the detours. Berserking is a 10-second casting-speed cooldown for your hardest pull.

Rogue Fast, gear-dependent

Two daggers, Sinister Strike, and no downtime once poisons arrive at 20 (Shenthul's chain in the Cleft of Shadow and the Sludge Fen). Regeneration and Throwing Specialization are both rogue racials in Era; Rapid Regeneration in Forever is a bandage that costs no cloth. Run Wailing Caverns and Razorfen Kraul for daggers.

Shaman Fast / safest

Totems arrive by quest, not by trainer: Earth at 4 (Canaga Earthcaller in the valley), Fire at 10 (Searn Firewarder in Grommash Hold or Swart in Sen'jin), Water at 20, Air at 30. Ghost Wolf at 20 is a mount you already own, Windfury at 30 turns a two-hander into the best leveling weapon in the game, and Healing Wave means you never sit to eat. Berserking speeds the melee swings too.

Priest Medium

Slow at 1–10, then Mind Blast, Shadow Word: Pain and a wand carry you. Trolls have the best priest racials in the game: Hex of Weakness at 10 (Ur'kyo in the Valley of Spirits) and Shadowguard at 20, both quests, not purchases. Shadowguard on a warrior friend in a dungeon pays your repair bill. The Sunken Temple chain at 50 hands you Blessed Prayer Beads.

Warrior Slowest

Gear-dependent and downtime-heavy; Regeneration and Berserking are the two racials that soften it, and Rapid Regeneration in Forever fixes it outright. Stances come by quest: Defensive at 10 (Veteran Uzzek at Far Watch Post), Berserker at 30 (Fray Island) alongside the Whirlwind weapon chain. Run Wailing Caverns and Razorfen Kraul for weapons, buy Heavy Linen Bandages by the stack.

Warlock Forever Fast, unpublished

New in WoW Forever. No Troll warlock quest text has been published: the steps on this page use the Orc chain from Durotar (Ruzan's imp at 4, Gan'rul Bloodeye's Voidwalker at 10, the Cazul and Dogran succubus chain at 20, Strahad Farsan's felhunter at 30, Felsteed at 40) on the assumption that a Troll starts in the same valley and uses the same Cleft of Shadow trainers. Verify each in the beta before trusting it.

Forever

Trolls keep their six Era classes and gain Warlock in Forever (the other new combinations are Undead paladin, Dwarf shaman, Orc mage, Human hunter, Gnome priest). Every talent tree is reworked, so the speed ranking above is Era's and may reshuffle.

Rules of the road

Hearthstone policy

  • Bind it where the page tells you, and nowhere else. Every bracket has one hearth and one flight point to collect.
  • Orgrimmar is the Horde's zeppelin hub, which is the one advantage a Troll has over a Tauren: every Eastern Kingdoms leg from 30 on is a hearth to the questing hub and a flight to Orgrimmar away. Bind at Razor Hill 7–12, the Crossroads and Camp Taurajo 12–20, then the hub you are questing from.
  • Log out in an inn or a capital. Rested XP doubles kill experience; a night's log-off is worth a bar and a half early on.

Kill order

  • Kill everything green or yellow on the way to an objective. Red is downtime, grey is nothing.
  • Turn in quests in batches. Walk the hub once per loop, not once per quest.
  • Skip escorts that fail on a bad pull, group quests you cannot solo two levels late, and anything that sends you across an ocean for a single turn-in.

Troll-specific habits

  • Bind a key to Berserking on day one and use it on cooldown against anything named. In Forever bind Rapid Regeneration next to it and channel it after every fight that left you under half.
  • Learn First Aid from Rawrk in Razor Hill. Linen from the Kul Tiras humans at Tiragarde pays for it; bandages are your only emergency button until 20.
  • Skinning plus a second gathering skill (Herbalism for a shaman or priest, Mining for a warrior or rogue) from Razor Hill onward. Trolls have no profession racial, so pick for gold, not skill points.
  • Class quests are in the steps, shaded and tagged. Do them when they appear: each one is a spell, pet or stance you cannot buy, and the level-10 ones (stance, pet, Fire totem, Hex of Weakness, Voidwalker) are the single biggest speed jump in the route.

Gold

  • Sell everything grey. Vendor whites that are not your weapon upgrade.
  • Mount money at 40 is 100 gold: 20 for Apprentice Riding from Xar'Ti and 80 for an Emerald, Turquoise or Violet Raptor from Zjolnir, both in Sen'jin Village. Shamans have Ghost Wolf at 20 and can push the raptor to 45 if gold is short; warlocks in Forever get the Felsteed free.
  • Buy weapons, not armor. A green weapon two levels above yours is the one purchase that pays back.

Before you move

Turn on Auto Loot in the Interface options. Bind a key to Berserking. Read the class scroll you spawn with: it is a free turn-in at your trainer. The valley is one loop: the Den, the boar farms north, the scorpid field and Hana'zua to the north-west, the cactus apples on the way, the peon camp east, the Burning Blade cave in the north, back to the Den.

Leave at 6 with Report to Sen'jin Village and A Peon's Burden in the log. East out of the valley onto the Durotar road, then south along the coast; Sen'jin Village is the troll huts on the shore.

Forever

Nothing announced touches the Valley of Trials. Troll warlocks are new in Forever; the Orc starter chain (Ruzan's imp, Gan'rul's Voidwalker) is the assumption on this page until the beta shows otherwise. Rapid Regeneration should be usable from level 1 verify; channel it after the Sarkoth fight and you will see why it matters.

First stop

Far Watch Post before anything else: Kargal Battlescar takes Conscript of the Horde and gives Crossroads Conscription (to Sergra Darkthorn, 910 XP for a walk), Ak'Zeloth takes Neeru's letter and gives The Demon Seed, and warriors find Uzzek under his tree just south-west of the post. Two ways to run it

Stranglethorn has the most quests of any zone but they spread from 30 to 45. Do the 30–35 layer now (Nesingwary's first two tiers, Grom'gol's troll war, Booty Bay's coastal jobs), go to Desolace for 32–35, and return to Stranglethorn at 37 and again at 42 as the higher tiers open. Buy food and water in Grom'gol before you leave it; the jungle sells nothing between camps. Every Nesingwary target is a beast: Beast Slaying makes this the best zone in the game for a Troll.

Leave at 35. Fly Shadowprey → Orgrimmar, zeppelin to Undercity, fly to Tarren Mill, road east into Arathi. Rebind at Hammerfall. This is the one leg that runs through Undercity: Hammerfall has no flight point until you have walked to it.

Orgrimmar logistics

Getting around the city

  • Valley of Strength (inside the main gate): bank, auction house, the inn (Innkeeper Gryshka), and the flight master Doras on the upper tier verify. Everything you need between quests is within sight of the gate.
  • Valley of Honor (north-east): the Hall of the Brave for warriors (Grezz Ragefist, Sorek, Zel'mak 80,31), Hunter's Hall for hunters (Ormak Grimshot, Sian'dur, Xor'juul 66,18, pet trainer Xao'tsu), the weapon masters Hanashi and Sayoc at Arms of Legend 82,20, the Ring of Valor.
  • Valley of Wisdom (north-west): Grommash Hold with Thrall and Nazgrel, and the shaman trainers Kardris Dreamseeker, Sagorne Creststrider, Sian'tsu and Searn Firewarder 39,36. Not the Valley of Spirits, whatever the name suggests.
  • Valley of Spirits (south-west, the troll district): the Spirit Lodge for priests (Ur'kyo, X'yera Al'tenas, Zayus 36,88) and Darkbriar Lodge for mages (Uthel'nay, Deino, Enyo, Thuul 39,86). Zankaja, the warlock succubus contact, stands on the rise between here and the Valley of Wisdom 37,60.
  • Cleft of Shadow (the cave under the Drag): the Shadowswift Brotherhood for rogues (Shenthul, Ormok, Gest, Therzok 43,54), the Darkfire Enclave for warlocks (Gan'rul Bloodeye, Zevrost, Grol'dar, Kurgul 47,50), Neeru Fireblade, Kor'ghan, and the Ragefire Chasm entrance.
  • The Drag holds most profession trainers; First Aid is with Arnok in the Valley of Honor verify. Cooking is in the Drag.

Zeppelins and boats

  • The zeppelin tower stands just outside the main gate: platforms for Undercity and Grom'gol. Doras is inside the gate, so a flight in and a zeppelin out is a two-minute walk.
  • Ratchet ↔ Booty Bay boat links the Barrens to Stranglethorn without a zeppelin.
  • Undercity's tower returns to Orgrimmar and Grom'gol; you use it for Tarren Mill, Kargath, Stonard, Revantusk and the Plaguelands flights only.
  • Thunder Bluff is optional for a Troll: Apothecary Zamah's delivery from the Crossroads gets you the flight point if you want the Razorfen and Scarlet Monastery quests it holds.

Mount and gold

  • Apprentice Riding from Xar'Ti and an Emerald, Turquoise or Violet Raptor from Zjolnir, both in Sen'jin Village, not Orgrimmar. Together 100 gold at level 40 before reputation discounts. Fly Orgrimmar and ride the road south through Razor Hill; twelve minutes each way.
  • Shamans have Ghost Wolf at 20 (40% speed, usable indoors, instant with the talent). It does not replace a raptor at 60, but it lets you spend the 100 gold on weapons first and buy the raptor at 43–45 when the money is easy. Warlocks in Forever get the Felsteed free at 40 and the Dreadsteed for about 150 gold of materials at 60.
  • Realistic gold by 40 on this route with Skinning plus a gathering skill and nothing bought but weapons: 130–170. Without gathering: 70–90, which is why the 38–40 bracket sits in Dustwallow, where the dragonkin drop vendor greens.
  • Epic Swift Blue, Olive or Orange Raptor at 60: 1,000 gold, 900 with Darkspear Trolls Exalted verify. Every Durotar quest counts toward that rep, as do Sen'jin's and Malaka'jin's, and the Barrens quests split between Orgrimmar and Darkspear.
