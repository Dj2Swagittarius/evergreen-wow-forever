The Violet Road

A hub-by-hub route for an Alliance Skyborne: Zephras Isle to level 12, the airship to Dalaran, Loch Modan to 20, then the Alliance road to 60, with the class quests for Warrior, Hunter, Mage, Rogue and Druid folded into the steps. Pick your class once and the page shows only yours. The Skyborne do not exist in Classic Era, so the whole page is beta data; the isle is tester reports, the Alliance landing is an inference, and everything not certain is flagged.

Start here

This is a route, not an encyclopedia. Each bracket tells you where to bind your hearthstone, which flight point to grab, and a numbered order of quests to run. Tick the boxes as you go; the page remembers them in this browser only.

Steps shaded in blue are class quests. Choose your class in Pick your class and the other four classes' steps disappear; the choice is remembered, and ?class=mage in the address bar does the same thing. With no class chosen every class step shows, each with a tag.

Two kinds of callouts appear throughout. Era would be the Classic Era baseline; the Skyborne have none, so it only appears in the racials table to say so. Forever marks what WoW Forever has publicly changed or added and what to re-check in the beta client. Where a coordinate, count or quest name comes from a tester's forum post or from memory rather than a checked source it is tagged verify; treat those as "the right area", not gospel. On Zephras Isle that is almost everything, and nobody has yet posted where the Alliance airship lands.

47,60 style numbers are map coordinates for the zone named in that step. Quest names are in bold. Coordinates were checked for Loch Modan, the Ironforge class chains and the level-50 chains; the 20–60 brackets are built from Boston's and Jame's Alliance guides with the TBC-only content removed and are hub-and-chain level.

The shape of the route: the High Order airship is inferred to land at rebuilt Dalaran in the Alterac Mountains, a 30ish area, so a level-12 Skyborne rides out to Southshore, flies to Ironforge and joins the Alliance road in Loch Modan, the nearest 10–20 zone that also feeds the Wetlands at 20. From 20 the route is the standard Alliance one: Eastern Kingdoms hubs with Menethil's boats to Kalimdor when the quests are there. Ironforge is the home city on this page; Stormwind is a free tram ride away and holds the mage and rogue quest givers.

Every race gets two active and two passive racials in Forever. Hunter Forever Fastest

A pet from level 10 and nothing ever touches you again. The isle has its own taming chain (three local winged beasts, which show as the Fox family once tamed verify), then Beast Training. Keep the pet fed, keep arrows stocked, and Read Ley Line between pulls so the pet's mend never waits on your mana.

Mage Forever Fast

Frostbolt, Frost Nova, repeat, and Read Ley Line makes the drinking that slows every other mage optional. The class quests are Ironforge's (Bink at 10 verify), then Stormwind's Manaweave Robe at 15 with its gems in Silver Stream Mine right on this route, the Astral Knot robe at 26, Tabetha's wand at 30–40, and Xylem at 50. The High Order's own class.

Druid Forever Medium, then fast

Slow until Bear Form at 10 (the Moonglade chain works for Skyborne per an Alliance tester; Lunaclaw is in Darkshore), steady until Cat Form at 20, then fast. Skyborne forms are sky-blue, feathered avian hybrids. Which druid trainer an Alliance Skyborne reports to is unconfirmed; this page routes through Mathrengyl Bearwalker in Darnassus verify, which makes the 14 and 16 chains a Darkshore trip.

Rogue Forever Medium

Stealth past everything, kill one thing at a time. The Alliance class chains are race-specific at 10 (this page assumes Ironforge's Road to Salvation verify), then shared: Alther's Mill at 16, Klaven's Tower for Poisons at 20, Ravenholdt at 50 for the Sunken Temple mask.

Warrior Forever Slowest

Gear-dependent and downtime-heavy, and the Skyborne bring no health racial to soften it; Read Ley Line at least halves the sitting. Stances come by quest: Defensive at 10 (Ironforge's Vejrek chain, assumed verify), Berserker at 30 (Fray Island) alongside the Whirlwind weapon chain. Run Deadmines and the Stockade for weapons, buy Heavy Linen Bandages by the stack.

Forever

All five combinations are new in Forever; there is no Era baseline for any of them. The speed ranking is the Era ranking of the same classes on other Alliance races and every talent tree is reworked, so it may reshuffle. Beta testers report standard Classic class chains for hunters and druids and no Skyborne-only class quest for anyone verify.

Rules of the road

Hearthstone policy

  • Bind it where the page tells you, and nowhere else. Every bracket has one hearth and one flight point to collect.
  • Your hearth starts bound on Zephras Isle (Thendal Village, presumably) verify. The isle is a place you leave; the first thing to do on the mainland is rebind, in Ironforge at the Stonefire Tavern, then at Thelsamar once you are questing there.
  • Ironforge is the hub of the Alliance flight net and the tram makes Stormwind free, so "bind in a capital" means Ironforge on this page unless a bracket says otherwise.
  • Log out in an inn or a capital. Rested XP doubles kill experience; a night's log-off is worth a bar and a half early on.

Kill order

  • Kill everything green or yellow on the way to an objective. Red is downtime, grey is nothing.
  • Turn in quests in batches. Walk the hub once per loop, not once per quest.
  • Skip escorts that fail on a bad pull, group quests you cannot solo two levels late, and anything that sends you across an ocean for a single turn-in.

Skyborne-specific habits

  • Bind Walk on Air and Read Ley Line on day one. Read Ley Line after every fight that cost you health or mana; the 2-second cast is shorter than sitting down.
  • Learn First Aid in Thendal or Valanaar if a trainer exists verify; otherwise from the Ironforge Physician in the Great Forge at 12. Bandages are your only emergency button until 20.
  • Skinning plus Herbalism from the isle onward, or Mining if you are a warrior. Nothing racial pushes a profession.
  • Class quests are in the steps, shaded and tagged. Do them when they appear: each one is a spell or form you cannot buy, and the level-10 ones (stance, pet, Blade of Cunning, Ley Staff, Bear Form) are the single biggest speed jump in the route.
  • You are a guest of the Alliance rather than a member in the isle's dialogue (the Horde side says so explicitly; the Alliance equivalent is unconfirmed verify). Ironforge and Stormwind reputation start at neutral either way.

Gold

  • Sell everything grey. Vendor whites that are not your weapon upgrade.
  • Mount money at 40 is 100 gold: 20 for Apprentice Riding and 80 for the mount. The Skyborne mount is the Galestrider, datamined as a reputation reward with no vendor found yet verify; the fallback is a ram from Veron Amberstill at Amberstill Ranch at Ironforge Exalted, or a horse or nightsaber at the matching city's Exalted. Druids have Travel Form at 30 and can push the purchase to 45.
  • Buy weapons, not armor. A green weapon two levels above yours is the one purchase that pays back.

Read first

No quest list for Zephras Isle has been published. Blizzard puts every zone at 50 to 200 quests; testers finish the isle at 13 to 14. What follows is the story order from beta reports, the two quest names that are public, and the subzone names from the beta map, split across the four settlements at levels that are an estimate verify. Take every quest a hub offers and clear it before moving on; the isle is linear and you will not miss anything by doing so.

Before you move

Turn on Auto Loot in the Interface options. Bind keys to Walk on Air and Read Ley Line. Read the class scroll you spawn with if there is one: it is a free turn-in at your trainer verify. The zone plays as a U: start here in the north-west, south and downhill through forest and farmland, then back up to the north-east and the finale.

Leave at 5, south into the Shen'dar Highlands. There is no breadcrumb between the villages; testers complained about it.

Forever

Blessing of Zephras (+40% run speed for 5 minutes, breaks on hostile action) is datamined for this zone; its source object is undocumented verify. Read Ley Line gets its 15-minute duration at special isle locations, so cast it near anything that looks like a ley line. Access to the isle requires the Skyborne Heroic Pack or higher.

Why Loch Modan

Dalaran and the Alterac wilds are 28–33 content; a level-12 cannot quest there. Of the Alliance 10–20 zones, Loch Modan is one flight from Ironforge, its north road drops straight into the Wetlands at 20 where the Alliance 20–25 bracket starts, and Ironforge holds the class-quest NPCs this page assumes for a Skyborne plus the tram to Stormwind. Darkshore would mean a Menethil boat and a Kalimdor start away from the 20–25 hubs; Westfall a longer Stormwind detour that ends in Redridge, which the route reaches later anyway. If Forever gives Dalaran a flight master or a breadcrumb quest to a new 12–20 Alliance area, follow that instead verify.

Leave at 20. North past Algaz Station through Dun Algaz into the Wetlands, then north and west to Menethil Harbor. Rebind there.

Forever

Nothing announced touches Loch Modan. The Wetlands expansion, new content north of here, is the thing to watch at 20; it may add a hub between Dun Algaz and Menethil. Dalaran itself is a 30ish city with its own quests and the 28–33 City of Dalaran dungeon; you come back for it in the 30–35 bracket.

Ironforge and Stormwind logistics

Getting around Ironforge

  • The Commons (inside the front gate): the Stonefire Tavern (Innkeeper Firebrew), bank and auction house. Bind here at 12.
  • The Great Forge (centre): Gryth Thurden the gryphon master 55,48, the Physician for First Aid 55,58, Tormus Deepforge for the warrior weapon chain verify.
  • Military Ward (south-east): the Hall of Arms with the warrior trainers and Muren Stormpike 66,89, the hunter trainers and Belia Thundergranite the pet trainer 71,85, Timberline Arms weapon masters.
  • Forlorn Cavern (north): rogue trainers and Hulfdan Blackbeard 52,15.
  • Mystic Ward (north-west): the Hall of Mysteries with the mage trainers and Bink 27,8, Talvash del Kissel for the Uldaman necklace.
  • Hall of Explorers (east): Prospector Stormpike, Historian Karnik, Librarian Mae Paledust, Roetten Stonehammer: the Loch Modan, Uldaman, Monastery and Desolace turn-ins. Tinker Town beside it: the Deeprun Tram and the Gnomeregan quests. No druid trainer in the city; Darnassus' Cenarion Enclave is yours.

Stormwind, the tram and the boats

  • The Deeprun Tram (Tinker Town ↔ Dwarven District) is free and takes a minute. Stormwind holds the rogue and mage quest givers: SI:7 in Old Town 76,60, the Wizard's Sanctum and the Blue Recluse in the Mage Quarter, Larson Clothiers. Flight master Dungar Longdrink in the Trade District.
  • Menethil Harbor has both Kalimdor boats: Auberdine (Darkshore, for Ashenvale, Stonetalon and Desolace) and Theramore (Dustwallow, for Tabetha and Gadgetzan's flight net). Auberdine ↔ Rut'theran links to Darnassus.
  • Booty Bay ↔ Ratchet is the neutral boat: Fray Island for warriors, the Barrens for druids' Islen Waterseer, and a back door into Horde Kalimdor.

Mount and gold

  • The Skyborne racial mount family is the Galestrider: Empyrean, Regal, Stormy and Umber at the level-40 60% tier, Swift versions at 60. They are listed as reputation rewards in the beta data, presumably High Order reputation for the Alliance variant; no vendor, price or reputation rank has been found verify. Riding is TBC-style: Apprentice at 40, epic at 60.
  • If the Galestrider is not buyable at 40, the Classic rule applies: any Alliance race's mount at Exalted with that race's city. Ironforge is the reputation this route feeds (Loch Modan, Wetlands, the Badlands and Searing Gorge dwarves), so Veron Amberstill's rams at Amberstill Ranch in Dun Morogh are the fallback, with Ultham Ironhorn next to him for riding. 100 gold total.
  • Druids have Travel Form at 30, which lets them spend the 100 gold on weapons first and buy the mount at 43–45 when the money is easy. Everyone else wants the mount the day they hit 40.
  • The Cerulean Prideclaw, the ground mount in the Skyborne packs, is usable by all races; whether it needs riding skill at 40 or is a free mount is unreported verify.
  • Realistic gold by 40 on this route with Herbalism plus Skinning and nothing bought but weapons: 130–170. Without gathering: 70–90, which is why the 38–40 bracket sits in Dustwallow and Stranglethorn, where the Missing Diplomat pays and the beast tiers drop vendor greens.
  • Epic mount at 60: 1,000 gold, 900 at Exalted with whichever reputation sells it.
