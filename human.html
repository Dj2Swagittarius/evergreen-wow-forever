The King's Road

A hub-by-hub Human leveling route from Northshire Abbey to level 60, with the class quests for Warrior, Paladin, Rogue, Priest, Mage and Warlock folded into the steps, and the Forever-only Human Hunter marked where it will slot in. Pick your class once and the page shows only yours. Built on Classic Era rules; everything WoW Forever is known to change is flagged in violet.

Start here

This is a route, not an encyclopedia. Each bracket tells you where to bind your hearthstone, which flight point to grab, and a numbered order of quests to run. Tick the boxes as you go; the page remembers them in this browser only.

Steps shaded in blue are class quests. Choose your class in Pick your class and the other classes' steps disappear; the choice is remembered, and ?class=paladin in the address bar does the same thing. With no class chosen every class step shows, each with a tag. Hunter is a Forever-only Human class: its button is there, but its low-level quests are unpublished and only the faction-wide Sunken Temple chain is listed.

Two kinds of callouts appear throughout. Era is the verified Classic Era baseline. Forever marks what WoW Forever has publicly changed or added, and what to re-check once the beta is playable. Where a coordinate or count comes from memory rather than a checked source it is tagged verify; treat those as "the right area", not gospel.

47,60 style numbers are map coordinates for the zone named in that step. Quest names are in bold. Coordinates were checked for Elwynn, Westfall, Redridge and the class chains; elsewhere they are close enough to find the NPC with the map open.

The route stays in the Eastern Kingdoms as long as the quests hold out, because a Human's hearth, trainers and flight net all sit between Stormwind and Menethil Harbor. Kalimdor legs start at 23 (a boat to Auberdine for Ashenvale) and again at 30 (Thousand Needles by way of Theramore); the Deeprun Tram makes Ironforge a free extension of Stormwind for trainers and turn-ins.

Pick your class

Solo leveling speed, 1–60
Show quests for

Showing every class quest. Pick one and the other classes' steps are hidden everywhere on the page; the Evergreen addon does the same in-game from your character's class.

Warlock Fastest

An Imp at 4 from the abbey, a Voidwalker at 10 from the Slaughtered Lamb, and from then on the pet tanks while you Drain Life and never sit. Succubus at 20 (a Barrens and Ashenvale trip), Felhunter at 30 from Strahad in Ratchet, a free Felsteed at 40. The Human Spirit helps a Life Tap class less than others, but it never hurts.

Mage Fast

Free food and water, Frost Nova plus Frostbolt kiting, and the most class quests of any Alliance class: the Ley Staff at 10 (Mirror Lake), the Manaweave Robe at 15, the Astral Knot Garment at 26, then Tabetha's wand chain at 30–40 and the Celestial Orb at 40. Stormwind's Wizard's Sanctum is your second home.

Rogue Fast

Ambush, Sinister Strike, and a sword in each hand: Sword Specialization makes a Human rogue the best-hitting rogue in Era. Blade of Cunning at 10 from SI:7, lockpicking practice at 16 at Alther's Mill, Poisons at 20 through Klaven's Tower on the Westfall border. Bandages and Evasion cover the pulls that go wrong.

Hunter Forever Fastest, unpublished

New Human and class combination in WoW Forever. No Human taming chain has been documented from the beta; the Stormwind hunter trainers Einris Brightspear and Ulfir Ironbeard already exist in Classic and are the likely Training the Beast targets. Everything before the level-50 Sunken Temple chain is unpublished. Treat the button as a placeholder until the client is playable. verify

Paladin Medium / safest

Never dies, never hurries. Redemption by quest at 12 (Duthorian Rall in the Cathedral), Sense Undead and the Bastion of Stormwind at 20, then Verigan's Fist at 22, the best two-hander a leveling paladin will hold until 40, when the Warhorse is free. Seal of the Crusader plus Judgement, a two-handed mace or sword for the racial, and Consecration when it comes.

Priest Medium

Wand, Shadow Word: Pain, Mind Blast, and Power Word: Shield keep downtime low once Spirit Tap arrives. Human priests get Desperate Prayer at 10 and Feedback at 20, both talk-and-learn quests. Class quests are otherwise thin until Cenarion Aid at 50, so nothing pulls you off the zone route.

Warrior Slowest

Gear-dependent and downtime-heavy, but Sword Specialization is exactly the racial a warrior wants. Defensive Stance at 10 by beating Bartleby in the Pig and Whistle, then Haggard's weapon from Dead-tooth Jack; Fire Hardened armor pieces at 28–30; Berserker Stance at 30 on Fray Island and the Whirlwind weapon chain at 35–40. Run the Deadmines and the Stockade for weapons, buy bandages by the stack.

Forever

Humans keep these six classes in Forever and gain Hunter; the other new combinations go elsewhere (Undead paladin, Dwarf shaman, Gnome priest, Night Elf mage, Troll warlock, Orc mage). Every talent tree is reworked, so the speed ranking above is Era's and may reshuffle. Fear Ward becomes baseline for every priest.

Rules of the road

Hearthstone policy

  • Bind it where the page tells you, and nowhere else. Every bracket has one hearth and one flight point to collect.
  • Stormwind is the centre of the Alliance flight net and the Deeprun Tram makes Ironforge part of it. Bind in the field hub you are questing from; hearth to the capital only when a class quest or a trainer visit is due, then fly back out.
  • Log out in an inn or a capital. Rested XP doubles kill experience; a night's log-off is worth a bar and a half early on.

Kill order

  • Kill everything green or yellow on the way to an objective. Red is downtime, grey is nothing.
  • Turn in quests in batches. Walk the hub once per loop, not once per quest.
  • Skip escorts that fail on a bad pull, group quests you cannot solo two levels late, and anything that sends you across an ocean for a single turn-in.

Human-specific habits

  • Buy swords, or maces if a sword is not on offer. The +5 skill is the racial; a green sword two levels above you beats a blue axe. In Forever only swords keep the bonus.
  • Learn First Aid in Goldshire from Physician Michelle Belle, upstairs in the Lion's Pride Inn. Linen from the Defias pays for it; bandages are your only emergency button until 20.
  • Skinning plus a gathering profession from Goldshire onward. Elwynn and Westfall are thick with wolves, boars and bears, and Skinning is gold with no detours. Mining suits the Elwynn and Redridge mines; Herbalism suits Westfall and Duskwood.
  • Class quests are in the steps, shaded and tagged. Do them when they appear: each one is a spell, pet or weapon you cannot buy, and the level-10 ones (Defensive Stance, Voidwalker, Blade of Cunning, the Ley Staff, Redemption at 12) are the single biggest speed jump in the route.

Gold

  • Sell everything grey. Vendor whites that are not your weapon upgrade.
  • Mount money at 40 is 100 gold: 20 for Apprentice Riding from Randal Hunter and 80 for a Pinto, Chestnut or Brown horse from Katie Hunter, both at Eastvale Logging Camp in Elwynn. Paladins get the Warhorse and warlocks the Felsteed free at 40, so both can spend the 100 gold on weapons.
  • Buy weapons, not armor. A green weapon two levels above yours is the one purchase that pays back.

Before you move

Turn on Auto Loot in the Interface options. The class scroll you spawn with is a free turn-in at your trainer inside the abbey. Warlocks need 1 silver 10 copper on hand for the Imp. The valley is two loops: the kobold ridge north of the abbey and the vineyards east across the river. Vendor and repair at the wagon 47,42.

Leave at 5 with Report to Goldshire and Rest and Relaxation in the log; kill wolves west of the road on the way so you hit 6 in town.

Forever

Nothing announced touches Northshire. If a Human Hunter starts here in Forever, expect a hunter trainer in the abbey and a taming chain somewhere in Elwynn; none is published yet.

Two ways to run it

Stranglethorn has the most quests of any zone but they spread from 30 to 45. Do the Shimmering Flats first (flat ground, no travel, 30–32), then Stranglethorn's 30–35 layer (Nesingwary's first tiers, the Rebel Camp, Booty Bay's coastal jobs), Hillsbrad at 34–35, and Desolace whenever the jungle gets thin. Buy food and water in Booty Bay before you leave it; the jungle sells nothing between camps.

Leave at 35. Fly Southshore, ride north over the Thandol Span into Arathi, or fly Menethil → Refuge Pointe once you have it. Refuge Pointe has no inn in Era: keep the hearth at Southshore.

Forever

Nothing announced touches Stranglethorn, Thousand Needles or Desolace for the Alliance. Shen'Dralas, the new elven-ruins zone, opens off the Valley of Bones in southern Desolace with no published level range; if it is 30–35 it replaces the Desolace swing and Nijel's Point is the door.

Stormwind logistics

Getting around the city

  • Trade District (inside the gate): bank, auction house, the gryphon master Dungar Longdrink on the east edge 66,62, the Gilded Rose inn (Innkeeper Allison), Stephanie Turner and Roberto Pupellyverbos for the errands above.
  • Old Town (east): SI:7 and the rogue trainers 76,60; the Pig and Whistle Tavern and the warrior trainers 76,53; the Barracks with Doc Mixilpixil's cellar.
  • Cathedral Square (north): the Cathedral of Light 38,27 holds the paladin trainers (Duthorian Rall, Lord Grayson Shadowbreaker) and the priest trainers (Nara Meideros, Brother Joshua, High Priestess Laurena); Baros Alexston, Thomas and Mazen Mac'Nadir stand outside.
  • Mage Quarter (south-west): the Wizard's Sanctum and the mage trainers 39,82, the Slaughtered Lamb cellar and the warlock trainers 27,77, the Blue Recluse, Larson Clothiers, Collin Mauren.
  • Dwarven District (north-east): Grimand Elmore, Brohann Caskbelly, Wilder Thistlenettle and Shoni the Shilent, Furen Longbeard, and the Deeprun Tram entrance. Hunter trainers Einris Brightspear and Ulfir Ironbeard stand in the Dwarven District too, waiting for Forever verify.
  • Stormwind Keep (north-east): Highlord Bolvar Fordragon and Lady Katrana Prestor for the Onyxia chain, Major Samuelson, Bishop DeLavey. Professions cluster in the Trade District and the Mage Quarter; First Aid is in the Cathedral Square infirmary.

Tram and boats

  • The Deeprun Tram runs Dwarven District ↔ Tinker Town, Ironforge, free and about a minute. Every "go to Ironforge" in this guide means the Tram: the Hall of Explorers, the Mystic Ward, High Priest Rohan, Jordan Stilwell at the gate.
  • Menethil Harbor (Wetlands) is the Alliance port: the Bravery to Auberdine (Darkshore) and the Lady Mehley to Theramore (Dustwallow). Fly Stormwind → Ironforge → Menethil, or straight to Menethil once you have it.
  • Auberdine ↔ Rut'theran (the Moonspray) reaches Darnassus; Booty Bay ↔ Ratchet (the Maiden's Fancy, neutral) links Stranglethorn to the Barrens without a boat to Kalimdor's north. Stormwind Harbor does not exist in Era.

Mount and gold

  • Apprentice Riding from Randal Hunter and the Pinto, Chestnut Mare or Brown Horse from Katie Hunter, both at Eastvale Logging Camp in eastern Elwynn 84,69 verify, not in Stormwind. Together 100 gold at level 40 before reputation discounts, and Diplomacy pushes Stormwind rep toward the discount faster than any other race gets it.
  • Paladins learn Summon Warhorse from Duthorian Rall at 40 and warlocks Summon Felsteed from Strahad Farsan at 40, both free; only the 20-gold riding lesson is left to pay. Both classes spend the horse money on weapons.
  • Realistic gold by 40 on this route with Skinning plus a gathering profession and nothing bought but weapons: 130–170. Without gathering: 70–90, which is why the 38–40 leg sits in Dustwallow and Stranglethorn, where the humanoids drop cloth and vendor greens.
  • Epic Swift Palomino, Swift White Steed or Swift Brown Steed at 60: 1,000 gold, 900 with Stormwind Exalted. Every Elwynn, Westfall, Redridge and Duskwood quest counts toward that rep, as do the Stormwind-tagged chains in the Wetlands and Dustwallow.
