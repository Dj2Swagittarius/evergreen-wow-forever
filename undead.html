The Forsaken Road

A hub-by-hub Undead leveling route from the Deathknell crypt to level 60, with the class quests for Warrior, Rogue, Priest, Mage and Warlock folded into the steps. Pick your class once and the page shows only yours. Built on Classic Era rules, with every place WoW Forever is known to differ flagged in violet so the route can be re-cut once the beta settles.

Start here

This is a route, not an encyclopedia. Each bracket tells you where to bind your hearthstone, which flight point to grab, and a numbered order of quests to run. Tick the boxes as you go; the page remembers them in this browser only.

Steps shaded in green are class quests. Choose your class in Pick your class and the other classes' steps disappear; the choice is remembered, and ?class=warlock in the address bar does the same thing. With no class chosen every class step shows, each with a tag.

Two kinds of callouts appear throughout. Era is the verified Classic Era baseline. Forever marks what WoW Forever has publicly changed or added, and what to re-check once the beta is playable. Where a coordinate or count in the 30–60 half comes from memory rather than a checked source it is tagged verify; treat those as "the right area", not gospel.

61,52 style numbers are map coordinates for the zone named in that step. Quest names are in bold. Coordinates were checked for Tirisfal, Silverpine and Hillsbrad; elsewhere they are close enough to find the NPC with the map open.

The route leans on Eastern Kingdoms hubs because a Forsaken lives next to two zeppelin towers. Kalimdor swings happen only where the XP density is clearly better: the Barrens at 17–20, Thousand Needles at 25–30, and the 45–55 desert and crater zones.

Pick your class

Solo leveling speed, 1–60
Show quests for

Showing every class quest. Pick one and the other classes' steps are hidden everywhere on the page; the Evergreen addon does the same in-game from your character's class.

Mage Fastest

Frost from 20 on: Frostbolt, Frost Nova, Blizzard AoE pulls from 30+. Conjured food and water plus Cannibalize mean zero vendor spend. Free teleports to Undercity and Orgrimmar from 20 and 30 save hours of travel on this route. Class quests: Anastasia Hartwell's errands at 10 and 15, the Nether-lace robe at 26, Tabetha's wand at 40, Xylem at 50.

Warlock Fastest / safest

Voidwalker tanks from 10, Affliction dots let you fight two mobs at once, Drain Life and Cannibalize erase downtime. Free mount at 40 (Felsteed) saves 100 gold. Undead warlocks get the Voidwalker quest from Carendin Halgar in the Magic Quarter; every later pet (Succubus 20, Felhunter 30) and both mounts are quests too.

Rogue Fast

Highest single-target output early, Stealth skips trash on escort and pickup quests, and Cannibalize covers the first-aid gap. Mennet Carkad in the Rogues' Quarter starts the level-10 Deathstalkers chain (Astor Hadren in Silverpine) and the level-20 poison quest. Weak against packs; pull one at a time.

Priest Medium

Shadow Word: Pain, wand, Cannibalize. Undead priests get two racial spells by trainer quest: Touch of Weakness at 10 and Devouring Plague (a big dot on a 3 min cooldown) at 20. Spirit Tap at 10 keeps mana up. Slow until wands at 5 and Mind Flay at 20, then steady.

Warrior Slowest

Gear-dependent and downtime-heavy. Cannibalize helps more here than anywhere. Stances come by quest: Defensive at 10 (Veteran Uzzek, reached on the Barrens leg at 17), Berserker at 30 (Fray Island) alongside the Whirlwind weapon chain from Bath'rah in Alterac, next door to Tarren Mill. Run Wailing Caverns, Shadowfang Keep and Razorfen Kraul for weapon upgrades, keep a two-hander, and buy Heavy Linen Bandages by the stack.

Paladin Forever only

Forever New Forsaken combination. Class hub is Bandarion Keep in the Whispering Wood of Tirisfal, with an Undead Warhorse at 40 and a Charger quest chain at 60. Holy Strike returns as a level-6 spell. No Era data exists; the only class quests published so far are the two Deathknell intro quests in the 1–6 bracket, and the rest will be added after the beta.

Forever

Forsaken keep all five Era classes in Forever and add the paladin. Every talent tree is reworked, so the speed ranking above is Era's and may reshuffle. Class quest chains (pets, stances, poisons, mounts) were not mentioned as changed; verify each at the level it appears.

Rules of the road

Hearthstone policy

  • Bind it where the page tells you, and nowhere else. Every bracket has one hearth and one flight point to collect.
  • Hearth is a free zeppelin ticket: bind at Sepulcher or Tarren Mill while you quest in Kalimdor and you skip the boat ride home.
  • Log out in an inn or a capital. Rested XP doubles kill experience; a night's log-off is worth a bar and a half early on.

Kill order

  • Kill everything green or yellow on the way to an objective. Red is downtime, grey is nothing.
  • Turn in quests in batches. Walk the hub once per loop, not once per quest.
  • Skip escorts that fail on a bad pull, group quests you cannot solo two levels late, and anything that sends you across an ocean for a single turn-in.

Undead-specific habits

  • Cannibalize after every humanoid or undead pull. Do not buy food until Stranglethorn, where the beasts do not leave edible corpses.
  • Learn First Aid at Brill (Nurse Neela). Linen from Tirisfal and Silverpine humanoids pays for it; keep a stack of bandages, they are your only emergency button until 20.
  • Will of the Forsaken before you pull Scarlet priests and warlocks. Fear breaks pulls into wipes at level 8.
  • Keep the level-10 Undercity trip short: train, do the Apothecarium turn-ins, learn a profession, leave.

Gold

  • Sell everything grey. Vendor whites that are not your weapon upgrade.
  • Skinning plus Mining or Herbalism from Brill onward. Mount money at 40 is 100 gold: 20 for Apprentice Riding, 80 for the Skeletal Horse from Zachariah in Brill.
  • Buy weapons, not armor. A green weapon two levels above yours is the one purchase that pays back.

Before you move

Turn on Auto Loot in the Interface options. Bind a key to your hearthstone later; for now, bind one to Cannibalize. Read the class scroll you spawn with: it is a free turn-in quest at your trainer.

Leave at level 5 with Vital Intelligence in the log. Follow the road east; Solliden Farmstead 37,50 is on the way and you will be back for its pumpkins.

Forever

Deathknell is the one zone most likely to be left alone. The new Forsaken paladin content sits in the Whispering Wood (Bandarion Keep), north of the normal road, so expect an extra branch at level 1 for paladins only.

Pickup sweep

Grab every quest in Brill in one lap: Executor Zygand and Magistrate Sevren in the town hall, Apothecary Johaan behind it, Deathguard Dillinger by the gate, Gretchen Dedmar, Junior Apothecary Holland, Coleman Farthing by the graveyard, the Wanted poster, and Gordo the abomination on the road east of town. Leave at 12 (13 if you finished the crypt) with Deaths in the Family, the Scarlet chain, Prodigal Lich Returns and Family Crypt turned in. Take the road south out of Brill into Silverpine.

Forever

A new 15–25 Horde zone on the Undercity surface, plus a Ruins of Lordaeron dungeon (reported 15–20), is being built for this exact stretch. If it ships as described, expect to bounce back here at 15 instead of leaving Tirisfal for good.

Watch for

Sons of Arugal patrol the roads north of the Sepulcher and around the Skittering Dark. They are level 20+ worgen and will kill a 13. Pull nothing while one is in view. Pyrewood Village turns into worgen at night, which is when you want it for the shackle quests, and not before 16.

Leave at 17–18. Hearth to the Sepulcher, fly or walk to Undercity, take the zeppelin to Orgrimmar. Keep the Sepulcher hearth for now.

Come back at 22–24

Shadowfang Keep is the best Forsaken dungeon in the game: Deathstalkers in Shadowfang (Hadrec), Arugal Must Die (Dalar), The Book of Ur (Keeper Bel'dugur, Undercity Apothecarium), plus Fenris Isle on the way. Do it once with a group during the Hillsbrad bracket.

Why bother

Silverpine runs dry at 17, Hillsbrad is thin below 20, and the Barrens has more level 16–20 quests around one inn than anywhere else on the Horde side. Three levels here also give you Orgrimmar's flight point and the Ratchet boat, which you need for Booty Bay later.

Leave at 20. Fly Crossroads → Orgrimmar, zeppelin to Undercity, fly to the Sepulcher, ride the road east into Hillsbrad. Rebind at Tarren Mill.

Two ways to run it

Stranglethorn has the most quests of any zone but they spread from 30 to 45. Do the 30–35 layer now (Nesingwary's first two tiers, Grom'gol's troll war, Booty Bay's coastal jobs), go to Desolace for 32–35, and return to Stranglethorn at 37 and again at 42 as the higher tiers open. Buy food here: beasts leave nothing to Cannibalize.

Leave at 35. Fly Shadowprey → Orgrimmar, zeppelin to Undercity, fly to Tarren Mill, road east into Arathi. Rebind at Hammerfall.

Undercity logistics

Getting around the city

  • Trade Quarter (center): bank, auction house, the flight master on the upper ring, most profession trainers.
  • Magic Quarter: mage and warlock trainers, Bethor Iceshard, Anastasia Hartwell, Carendin Halgar.
  • Rogues' Quarter: rogue trainers, Mennet Carkad, engineering.
  • War Quarter: warrior and priest trainers, First Aid.
  • Apothecarium: Faranell, Zinge, Keeper Bel'dugur, alchemy. Half your Tirisfal and Silverpine chains end here.
  • Royal Quarter: Varimathras and Lady Sylvanas. Into The Scarlet Monastery and The Ranger Lord's Behest start here.

Zeppelins and boats

  • Undercity's tower stands on the ruined courtyard's north side, outside the city proper. Two platforms: Orgrimmar and Grom'gol.
  • Orgrimmar's tower outside the main gate returns to Undercity and Grom'gol.
  • Ratchet ↔ Booty Bay boat links the Barrens to Stranglethorn.

Flight points to collect, in route order

Eastern Kingdoms: Undercity · Sepulcher · Tarren Mill · Grom'gol · Booty Bay · Hammerfall · Kargath · Stonard · Thorium Point · Revantusk · Flame Crest · The Bulwark · Light's Hope

Kalimdor: Orgrimmar · Crossroads · Ratchet · Splintertree · Zoram'gar · Sun Rock · Freewind · Camp Taurajo · Thunder Bluff · Shadowprey · Brackenwall · Gadgetzan · Camp Mojache · Bloodvenom · Everlook · Cenarion Hold

Class quest stops

  • Level 4: warlock Piercing the Veil (Maximillion, Deathknell) for the imp; priest In Favor of Darkness, Deathknell to Brill.
  • Level 10: every class. Warrior Veteran Uzzek from Deathguard Dillinger in Brill (done at Far Watch Post on the Barrens leg at 17); rogue Mennet Carkad in the Rogues' Quarter, then Andron Gant and Astor Hadren on the Silverpine road; mage Anastasia Hartwell in the Magic Quarter (Balnir Farmstead); warlock Carendin Halgar in the Magic Quarter (Voidwalker); priest Touch of Weakness from any trainer. Paladin (Forever): Bandarion Keep.
  • Levels 15 and 16: mage Spellfire Robes (Anastasia, Undercity); rogue Fenwick Thatros lockpicking practice.
  • Level 20: rogue poisons (Mennet Carkad to Shenthul in Orgrimmar, Sludge Fen, ending at Serge Hinott in Tarren Mill); warlock Succubus (Carendin); priest Devouring Plague; warrior Speak with Ruga at Camp Taurajo.
  • Levels 25 and 26: warlock Orb of Soran'ruk (Doan Karhan, southern Barrens; Blackfathom and Shadowfang drops); mage Nether-lace Garment (Deino in Orgrimmar, Xavian in Ashenvale).
  • Level 30: warrior The Islander (Fray Island) then the Whirlwind chain from Bath'rah in Alterac; warlock Felhunter (Strahad Farsan, Ratchet; Needles cave and Wetlands rods); mage Journey to the Marsh (Tabetha, Dustwallow) for the wand at 40.
  • Level 40: warlock Felsteed (Strahad, Ratchet); warrior Whirlwind Weapon (Bath'rah).
  • Level 50: every class has a Sunken Temple chain from its Undercity trainer: warrior Fallen Hero (Swamp of Sorrows), rogue Lord Jorach Ravenholdt (Alterac) then Xylem, priest Ogtinc (Azshara), mage Xylem (Azshara), warlock Impsy (Felwood).
  • Level 60: warlock Dreadsteed (Mor'zul Bloodbringer, Burning Steppes).

Mount and gold

  • Apprentice Riding from Velma Warnam and the Skeletal Horse from Zachariah, both in Brill, not Undercity. Together 100 gold at level 40 before reputation discounts.
  • Warlocks get the Felsteed free at 40 from Carendin Halgar; put the 100 gold toward Dreadsteed materials at 60.
  • Realistic gold by 40 on this route with Skinning plus Mining and nothing bought but weapons: 120–160. Without gathering: 70–90, which is why the 38–40 bracket sits in Dustwallow, where the dragonkin drop vendor greens.
  • Epic Skeletal Warhorse at 60: 1,000 gold, 900 with Undercity Exalted. Start the Plaguelands rep grinds early if you want the discount.
