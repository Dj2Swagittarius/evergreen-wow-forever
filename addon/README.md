# Evergreen addon

One addon, four modules (v0.5.0, Sep 26 2026: Everbuff and EverMove were folded in; their old folders are in `_retired/`).

| Module | id | Command | What |
|---|---|---|---|
| Guide | `guide` | `/eg` | Leveling route, waypoint arrow, quest tracking (everything below) |
| Buffs | `buffs` | `/eb` | Former Everbuff: scans nearby players for missing buffs, one button casts the next (`Modules/Buffs.lua`) |
| Move | `move` | `/emove` | Former EverMove: drag Blizzard windows anywhere, positions saved (`Modules/Move.lua`) |
| Reveal | `reveal` | `/eg reveal` | Shows unexplored areas on the world map. `/eg reveal tint` tints them blue, `/eg reveal info` prints data for the open map (`Modules/Reveal.lua`) |
| Journal | `journal` | `/ej` | Dungeon journal: every Classic dungeon and raid with bosses and loot, the dungeon's quests (giver, where, chain, rewards, done / in log), entrance and quest-giver markers on the world map (`Modules/Journal.lua`) |

`/eg modules` lists them; `/eg module <id> on|off` then `/reload` switches one. Settings in `EvergreenDB.modules`. Buffs and Move keep their own saved variables (`EverbuffDB`, `EverMoveDB`), now declared by Evergreen.

**Journal** (our take on the DungeonJournal addon): `/ej` opens it, `/ej <name>` jumps to a dungeon (`/ej dead`), `/ej pins` toggles the world map markers, `/ej clear` drops the journal's waypoint. Left: dungeons and raids by level, coloured for your level, with a search box that also matches boss and item names. Right: the **Loot** tab (every boss, items with real tooltips; shift-click links, ctrl-click tries on; faction-only drops filtered) and the **Quests** tab (level, faction, who gives it and where, who takes it, the quest before it, required level, reward icons, and whether this character has done it or has it in the log; filters for your faction/class and hiding done quests). "Go" and "Entrance" point the Evergreen arrow there (TomTom or the game's own waypoint if the guide module is off); the arrow goes back to the guide on arrival or with `/ej clear`. The world map shows a skull at each dungeon entrance and a "!" at givers of dungeon quests you can take now or soon; click either to open the journal there.
Data (`Modules/Journal_Data.lua`) is generated by `tools/build_journal.lua` from AtlasLootClassic (GPL-2, boss loot tables) and Questie's Classic database (quests, NPC spots, entrances, quest rewards): 32 instances, 369 bosses, 3,123 loot entries, 637 dungeon quests. It is Classic data: Forever-only dungeons (Ruins of Lordaeron and the rest) and Forever loot changes are not in these sources yet; the Forever paladin dungeon quests are added by hand in `Journal.lua`.

**Reveal data** (`Modules/Reveal_Data.lua`) is generated, not hand written: `python tools/build_reveal.py` reads the WoW Forever beta client's `WorldMapOverlay` and `WorldMapOverlayTile` DB2 tables straight from the local CASC store (via the Hearthbreak project's reader in `E:\Project HearthBreak\tools\forever-port\casc`, parsed by `tools/wdc5.py`), so it covers Forever-only zones too. Re-run after a beta patch. First build (1.60.1): 84 maps, 1073 areas, 8 empty overlays skipped.

Leveling companion for WoW Forever and Classic Era. Race- and faction-agnostic engine; routes are data.

## What it does

- **Tracks a route from your quest log.** Every step is a quest, a travel leg, a hearth bind, a flight point, or a level gate. Quest steps complete themselves when you turn the quest in; travel steps when you arrive; binds when your hearthstone location matches; flight points when you open the taxi map next to the flight master; level gates when you ding.
- **Tells you what to do next.** The "NOW" panel shows the first unfinished step in your bracket and its phase: pick up (giver name and coords), do (objective text and coords), or turn in.
- **Waypoints.** Two at once: the game's own in-world super-tracked pin (floating marker with distance, also on map and minimap) and a floating arrow that rotates with your facing. TomTom gets the same target if installed.
- **Follows your level.** The bracket switches automatically when your level enters the next range and the current one is done. `<` `>` override, `Auto` returns.
- **Picks a route for your character.** By race first, then faction. A route's starting chapters can be race-only; other races of that faction join at the first shared bracket. Click the route label in the title bar to cycle routes.
- **Racial cooldowns** for whatever race you are (Cannibalize, Blood Fury, Berserking, War Stomp, Stoneform, Shadowmeld, Escape Artist, Perception) in the footer, plus violet "Forever" notes that flag what WoW Forever changes.

Routes live one per file (`Routes_<Race>.lua`, listed in the TOC after `Data.lua`). Shared class chains are injected once: Horde warrior/hunter/shaman/druid by `Routes_Tauren.lua`, Horde rogue/mage/priest/warlock by `Routes_Troll.lua` (plus the Forsaken-only brackets in `Routes_Forsaken_Class.lua`), Alliance by `Routes_Human.lua`. Skyborne exist on both sides, so the picker prefers the route whose faction matches yours; the race file name Blizzard uses for them is unverified (`/eg route <id>` overrides).

Class quests are steps with `cls="DRUID"` (or a set of classes). The engine drops every other class's steps when it selects the route, so a Tauren druid sees the Bear Form chain and never sees Call of Fire. Class steps show a gold class label in the list.

**Forever race/class combos** (Human Hunter, Gnome Priest, Dwarf Shaman, Orc Mage, Troll Warlock, Undead Paladin) are added by `Routes_Forever_Combos.lua`, loaded after every route file. Steps there can also carry `race="Gnome"` (race file name, or a set) so a shared bracket shows a step only to that race's version of the class. The Undead Paladin chain (levels 1-26, 31 quests, real Forever quest ids) comes from Wowhead's Forever database; the rest is marked `(verify)` where Blizzard has published nothing. Research: `docs/research/forever-new-combos.md`, `docs/research/forever-forsaken-paladin.md`.

### Optimized route (prototype, Sep 27 2026)

`forsaken-opt` ("The Forsaken Road (optimized)") is the Forsaken route with levels 1-18 reordered by `tools/route_optimizer.py`; from 17 on it continues with the hand route. Pick it with `/eg route forsaken-opt` (`/eg route auto` goes back). It uses **split steps**: `act="accept"`, `act="do"` and `act="turnin"` break one quest into steps placed apart, so pickups batch at a hub and objectives in the same area are done together. A step without `act` is still the whole quest.

The optimizer puts every quest's pickup, objective and turn-in in world yards (zone maps from the Forever client's UiMapAssignment table), and searches for the order that levels fastest under a time model: running at 7 yd/s (hearthstone when it saves time), zone and city crossings, kill time and kill XP from the objective counts and mob levels (Questie), turn-in XP (Questie), grinding when the simulated level is below a quest's requirement. Rules it cannot break: pickup before objective before turn-in, chain prerequisites, and the level at which the hand route placed binds and flight points. Class quests are inserted per class. Modelled result for 1-18, all classes' common steps: 566 min if you follow the hand route but batch pickups yourself, 508 min optimized (running 142 -> 101 min); against the arrow followed literally (one whole quest at a time) it saves about three hours. It is a model: check it in game.

### Route audit and Questie corrections

`tools/audit_routes.lua` checks every quest step of every route against Questie's Classic database: prerequisites (missing from the route, or listed after the quest), giver and turn-in NPC names, coordinates (more than 3 map units from a real spawn, or on another map), and the level gate in front of the step. The report is `docs/research/route-audit.md`. It also writes `Evergreen/Routes_Questie_Fixes.lua`, which corrects the giver/turn-in coordinates, map and NPC name of steps at load time, but only where the quest id is certain and only while the step still has the coordinates the fix was made for (a hand edit wins). First run: 583 corrections (wrong NPC named in 306 cases, e.g. Piercing the Veil is given by Venya Marthand, not the trainer; Fields of Grief goes to Apothecary Johaan). Findings that remain are leads, not verdicts: Forever can move NPCs, and several quests sharing a name makes some matches low-confidence (marked in the report).

### Rebuild and test

After editing any route: `cd tools && ./rebuild.sh --deploy`. It regenerates the audit and fixes, re-exports and re-optimizes `forsaken-opt`, then runs `test_harness.lua` for every Forever race x class (62 runs, level 1-30) and copies the addon into the beta client if everything passed. The harness loads the whole addon against stubbed WoW APIs and has a bot play the route: it accepts, completes and turns in whatever the arrow says and fails if the guide ever stops advancing or skips a required quest.

Every Era race has its own route now; an unknown race falls back to its faction's first route. Add a route by appending to `ns.ROUTES` in `Data.lua`; the step format is documented at the top of that file.

## Install

1. Copy the `Evergreen` folder into the client's `Interface\AddOns\` folder. On this machine: `E:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_\Interface\AddOns\` (WoW Forever beta, build 1.60.1) or `..\_classic_era_\Interface\AddOns\` (Classic Era 1.15.9).
2. Restart the client or `/reload`.
3. TOC lists 16001 (Forever beta) and 11509 (Era). If the list still says out of date, tick "Load out of date AddOns".

The 1.60 Forever client uses secret values for some combat data; the addon guards every numeric read and disables a widget after repeated errors instead of spamming. `/eg debug` prints what it can and cannot see.

## Fixing a waypoint

Two ways. In game: walk to the right spot, `/eg fix` while that step is the current one (the arrow's target). The override is stored in `EvergreenDB.fixes` and used instead of the route data from then on. Permanently: open `Routes_<Race>.lua`, find the quest (`q="Name"`), and change the numbers in `g=` (giver), `o=` (objective) or `r=` (turn-in): `{x, y, "label", uiMapID}`, coordinates in map percent. `/eg fixes` prints the in-game overrides in a form you can paste. After editing, copy the folder into `Interface\AddOns\` again and `/reload`.

## Mouse

- Left-click a step: point the waypoint at it (click again to release).
- Right-click a step: mark it done or undone by hand.
- Shift-right-click a step: mark it and everything above it done. Use once when installing mid-leveling.
- Left-click the NOW panel: toggle the arrow. Right-click: mark the current step done.
- Minimap button: left-click window, right-click arrow.

## How quest tracking works

Every quest step is resolved to a Classic **quest id** at build time (`QuestIDs.lua`, generated from Questie's database by `tools/resolve_quests.lua`). At login the addon reads the character's full completed-quest history from the server and the current quest log, so:

- quests you finished long before installing are marked done immediately,
- quests in your log show the right phase (do / turn in) and the objective or turn-in target,
- travel, bind and flight-point steps before your furthest completed quest are marked "(passed)",
- quests you never did but have moved past are marked "(skipped)" and do not block the NOW step; right-click un-skips.

`/eg sync` re-reads history and prints the counts. Giver and turn-in coordinates come from the database (exact NPC or object spawn); objective coordinates are hand-picked unless marked "(verify)", in which case the database's spawn centre is used.

Chains that reuse a name (A New Plague ×4, At War With The Scarlet Crusade ×4, Battle of Hillsbrad ×7, ...) are ordered by the database's prerequisite links.

## Files

- `Evergreen.toc` — manifest.
- `Data.lua` — brackets, routes (`ns.ROUTES`), racial spell table. Edit this to change or add routes.
- `QuestIDs.lua` — generated: quest ids per name (chain order) and database coordinates. Regenerate after editing routes:
  `luajit tools/resolve_quests.lua <dir with Questie classicQuestDB/NpcDB/ObjectDB.lua> Evergreen`
- `Core.lua` — tracking engine, main window, arrow, native pin, minimap button, slash commands.
- `arrow.tga` — 108-frame sprite sheet of a shaded 3D arrow (TomTom style), generated by `../make_arrow_sheet.py`.
- `../tools/quest_query.lua` — look up quests in the Questie database by name pattern or id while editing routes.
- `Routes_Forever_Combos.lua` — class steps for Forever's new race/class combos.
- `Routes_Questie_Fixes.lua` — generated by `tools/audit_routes.lua`; giver/turn-in corrections applied at load.
- `Routes_Forsaken_Opt.lua` — generated by `tools/route_optimizer.py`; the `forsaken-opt` route.
- `Modules.lua`, `Modules/` — module registry, Buffs (Everbuff), Move (EverMove), Reveal (world map) and its generated data.
- `../tools/rebuild.sh` — audit, optimize, test, deploy. `../tools/itinerary.py` prints a generated route's legs for a class, to eyeball an optimizer run.
