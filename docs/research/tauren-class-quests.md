## WARRIOR

### 10 — Defensive Stance (Uzzek / Thun'grim)
1. Veteran Uzzek [10] — Krang Stonehoof (Bloodhoof warrior trainer) → speak with Uzzek, south-west of Far Watch Post next to a tree, Barrens ~62,31 (verify).
2. Path of Defense [10] — Uzzek: 5 Singed Scales from thunder lizards at Thunder Ridge, Durotar (cross the bridge from Far Watch Post, north; ridge entrance ~39,32 Durotar). Reward Defensive Stance, Sunder Armor, Taunt.
3. Thun'grim Firegaze [10] — Uzzek → Thun'grim atop Thorn Hill east of the Crossroads, Barrens 57,30 (path up starts ~55,32).
4. Forged Steel [10] — Thun'grim: loot the Stolen Iron Chest by a broken wagon NW of him, Barrens 55,26 (quilboar). Reward: Thun'grim's Axe/Mace/Dagger/Sword.

### 20–30 — Brutal Armor (Ruga / Thun'grim)
5. Speak with Ruga [20] — Torm Ragetotem (TB) → Ruga Ragetotem, Camp Taurajo ~44.5,59.5 (verify).
6. Trial at the Field of Giants [20] — Ruga: 5 Twitching Antennae from silithids in the Field of Giants south of Taurajo toward the Dustwallow road (~47–50,78–85 verify); 30-min timer from first drop. Reward Ruga's Bulwark.
7. Speak with Thun'grim [20] — Ruga → Thun'grim 57,30.
8. Brutal Armor [30, req 20] — Thun'grim: 15 Smoky Iron Ingots (kobolds, Stonetalon), 10 Powdered Azurite (Hillsbrad Miners, Azurelode Mine, Hillsbrad, verify), 10 Iron Bars, 1 Vial of Phlogiston (Roogug, Razorfen Kraul).
9. Brutal Hauberk [30] — immediate turn-in, reward chest.
Sub-chains from Thun'grim after Brutal Armor: Gauntlets (Ula'elek, Sen'jin Village 56,74 → Satyr Hooves: 7 Uncloven Satyr Hooves, NE Ashenvale satyrs Satyrnaar ~85,45 / Xavian ~65,33 verify → Brutal Gauntlets); Legguards (Velora Nitely, Trade Quarter Undercity → Dragonmaw Shinbones: 8 from Dragonmaw orcs at Angerfang Encampment, Wetlands 45.7,45.4, many "failed" drops → Brutal Legguards); Helm (Orm Stonehoof, Karn's Smithy TB → Chimaeric Horn: Galvanized Horn from the Chimaera Matriarch, 27–28, south end of the Charred Vale, SW Stonetalon → Brutal Helm).

### 30 — Berserker Stance (Fray Island)
10. The Islander [30] — Torm Ragetotem → Klannoc Macleod, Fray Island off the Barrens coast south of Ratchet ~68,48 (swim from the Merchant Coast sand bar).
11. The Affray [30] — step on the grate, kill the Affray Challenger waves ending with Big Will (33). Reward Berserker Stance + Intercept.

### 30–40 — Whirlwind weapon (Bath'rah)
12. The Windwatcher [30] — Klannoc → Bath'rah the Windwatcher, Alterac Mountains 80,67 (ruins on the east bank of the river NE of Tarren Mill).
13. Cyclonian [40, req 30] — 8 Liferoot, 30 Bloodscalp Tusks (Bloodscalp trolls, N Stranglethorn), 1 Essence of the Exile = 8 Burning Charms (Circle of West Binding, Arathi 26.5,30.0) + 8 Thundering Charms (Circle of Outer Binding 51.0,49.2) + 8 Cresting Charms (Circle of East Binding 66.9,31.1), combined at the cauldron by Bath'rah.
14. The Summoning [40] — summon Cyclonian (40 elite, stuns) at the altar by Bath'rah's hut; loot the Whirlwind Heart. Group.
15. Whirlwind Weapon [40] — Whirlwind Axe / Sword / Warhammer.

### 50 — Sunken Temple
16. A Troubled Spirit [52, req 50] — Torm Ragetotem → Fallen Hero of the Horde, Swamp of Sorrows 34,66 (road at the Blasted Lands border).
17. Warrior Kinship — 7 Helboar, Blasted Lands.
18. War on the Shadowsworn — 20 Shadowsworn Adepts, 10 Cultists, 20 Thugs, Blasted Lands (Altar of Storms / Dreadmaul Hold, verify).
19. Voodoo Feathers [52D] — Temple of Atal'Hakkar: Gasher, Loro, Hukku, Zul'Lor, Mijan, Zolo → 2 Amber, 2 Blue, 2 Green Voodoo Feathers. Reward Fury Visor / Diamond Flask / Razorsteel Shoulders.

## HUNTER

### 10 — Taming (Bloodhoof Village)
1. The Hunter's Path [10] — Lanka Farshot, Camp Narache 45.3,75.3 (verify starter) → Yaw Sharpmane, north edge of Bloodhoof Village ~47,57 (verify).
2–4. Taming the Beast ×3 — Yaw: Adult Plainstrider (central Mulgore, Stonebull Lake), Prairie Stalker (wolf, N/W Mulgore), Swoop (bird, NW Mulgore, Windfury Ridge / Golden Plains). Third one rewards Tame Beast, Call Pet, Dismiss Pet.
5. Training the Beast [10] — Yaw → Holt Thunderhorn, Hunter Rise TB 57.3,89.9. Reward Beast Training, Feed Pet, Revive Pet.

## SHAMAN

### 4 — Call of Earth (Camp Narache)
1. Call of Earth (1519) [4] — Seer Ravenfeather, Camp Narache 45.0,75.2: 2 Ritual Salve from Bristleback Shamans, Brambleblade Ravine east of camp ~59.1,77.4.
2. Call of Earth (1520) — Earth Sapta: Kodo Rock, Mulgore ~54,80 (dirt patch halfway between Narache and the ravine); drink, talk to the Minor Manifestation of Earth, get Rough Quartz.
3. Call of Earth (1521) — Rough Quartz to Ravenfeather. Reward Earth Totem + Stoneskin Totem.

### 10–13 — Call of Fire
4. Call of Fire [10] — Xanis Flameweaver, Spirit Rise TB (also Narm Skychaser, Bloodhoof) → Kranal Fiss, Grol'dom Farm north of the Crossroads, Barrens ~56,20 (verify).
5. → Telf Joolam, Shrine of the Dormant Flame atop the tallest mountain NW of the Valley of Trials, Durotar ~36–38,57–59 (verify); path up starts by the Southfury River at the SW tip of the Southfury Watershed.
6. — 1 Fire Tar (Razormane Mystic/Water Seeker/Thornweaver/Geomancer, central Barrens quilboar) + 1 Reagent Pouch (Burning Blade Cultists, Burning Blade Coven cave SW of Razor Hill, Durotar, verify).
7. — Fire Sapta: click the Brazier of the Dormant Flame, drink, defeat the Minor Manifestation of Fire, loot the Torch of the Dormant Flame.
8. — Torch to Telf Joolam (2984). Reward Fire Totem + Searing Totem.

### 20–23 — Call of Water
9. Call of Water [20] — Xanis Flameweaver → Islen Waterseer, fishing hut on the Barrens coast just south of Ratchet ~66,44 (verify).
10. → Brine, hill at the north end of Blackthorn Ridge, S Barrens 43.4,77.4.
11. — fill three waterskins: Brown at the pond below Brine's hut, Red at the Tarren Mill well (Hillsbrad 62,20), Blue at the Ruins of Stardust fountain, SW Ashenvale ~33,67. Reward Vial of Purest Water.
12. — vial to Islen. Reward Water Sapta.
13. (63) — Brazier of Everfount on the Silverpine coast west of the Sepulcher 38.3,44.6: drink, defeat the Corrupt Minor Manifestation of Water, loot its Bracers, place them + Remaining Drops on the brazier, talk to the cleansed Manifestation, get the Shard of Water.
14. (96) — shard to Islen. Reward Water Totem + Healing Stream Totem.

### 30 — Call of Air
15. Call of Air [30] — Xanis Flameweaver → Prate Cloudseer, the Weathered Nook, hidden cave on the northern cliffs of Thousand Needles 53.6,42.7 (east from the Great Lift). Reward Air Totem + Swift Wind (+30% run speed 1 h).

### 50 — Spirit Totem / Da Voodoo
16. Elemental Mastery [52, req 50] — capital shaman trainer: 1 each Elemental Air/Fire/Earth/Water → Bath'rah the Windwatcher, Alterac 80,67.
17. Spirit Totem — 8 Bloodshot Spider Eyes (Lurkers) + 8 Thick Black Claws (Diseased bears; Carrion Vultures drop both), Western Plaguelands ~34,59.
18. Da Voodoo [52D] — 2 Amber, 2 Blue, 2 Green Voodoo Feathers from the six Atal'Hakkar troll mini-bosses. Reward Azurite Fists / Enamored Water Spirit / Wildstaff.

## DRUID

### 10 — Bear Form
1. Heeding the Call [10] — Gennia Runetotem (Bloodhoof druid trainer) or Innkeeper Pala (TB) → Turak Runetotem, Elder Rise 76.5,27.2.
2. Moonglade — Teleport: Moonglade; Dendrite Starblaze, Nighthaven 56.2,30.4.
3. Great Bear Spirit — Moonglade 39.1,27.6 (west along the mountains), finish its dialogue, return.
4. Back to Thunder Bluff — Turak.
5. Body and Heart — Cenarion Lunardust on the Moonkin Stone west of Camp Taurajo near the Mulgore border, Barrens ~42,60 (verify). Kill Lunaclaw (12). Reward Bear Form.

### 14 — Cure Poison

### 16 — Aquatic Form
11. A Lesson to Learn — Turak → Dendrite. 12. Trial of the Sea Lion — two lockboxes: underwater at the Sludge Fen, N Barrens 56.7,8.3; ocean floor west of the derelict boat at North Tide's Run, Silverpine 29.6,29.5 (mind fatigue). Combine at the Shrine of Remulos. 14. Aquatic Form — pendant to Turak. Reward Aquatic Form + Aquarius Belt.

### 50 — Sunken Temple (Torwa Pathfinder)
15. Torwa Pathfinder [52, req 50] — Turak → Torwa, Un'Goro 72,76 (rock bluff by the waterfalls at the bottom of the Tanaris ramp). 16. ## Discrepancies
- Shaman NPC coords differ between guides; wiki values that match quest text are used (Kranal ~56,20; Prate 53.6,42.7; Everfount 38.3,44.6).
- Wiki coords for Uzzek, Islen, Camp Taurajo, Moonkin Stone are Cataclysm-map values; Classic estimates flagged.
- Whirlwind chain starts from Klannoc Macleod after The Affray, not from Thun'grim. "Iron Coral" is the Alliance warrior chain; "Indurium Ore" is a neutral Needles quest.
