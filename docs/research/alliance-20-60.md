# Classic Era (1.15) Alliance Leveling Route, 20–60 — Research Notes

Compiled 2026-09-19 from wow-pro (Boston's 20–30, Jame's Alliance Chapters I–III), warcraft.wiki.gg zone/dungeon/NPC pages, Warcraft Tavern, Icy Veins, classicdb.ch and Wowhead Classic quest pages. Joana's Classic Alliance chapters at joanasworld.com are behind a member paywall (`/classic/alliance-20-30.php` redirects to a login) and could not be read; the route skeleton below therefore follows Boston + Jame's, cross-checked against the wiki for Classic-era validity.

Conventions: coordinates are zone map `(x, y)`. `[nn]` = quest level. `D` = dungeon quest, `G` = group/elite, `E` = elite. Anything not confirmed against a Classic-specific source is marked **(verify)**.

Important source caveat: Boston's and Jame's guides were written for patch 2.x (TBC). Content they route through that does **not** exist in Classic Era has been flagged: Forest Song hub + flight path (added 2.1.0), Emerald Sanctuary / Rebel Camp / Mudsprocket flight paths and the Dustwallow Marsh expansion (Nat Pagle, Alcaz Island, Tabetha's Farm rework, Mudsprocket — all 2.3.0), and inn/innkeepers added in 4.0.3a (Refuge Pointe, Chillwind Camp, Nethergarde Keep, Talonbranch Glade, Stonetalon Peak).

Two premises in the brief that the sources contradict:
- **Trol'kalar is Horde-only in Classic.** Quest 645 "Trol'kalar" and the Sigil of Strom/Thoradin/Arathor/Trollbane chain are given by Zengu at Hammerfall (Darkspear reputation). The Alliance Arathi content at Refuge Pointe is Northfold Manor, Stromgarde Badges, the Tower of Arathor chain (Kryten/Skuerto), Hints of a New Plague and Arathi Basin PvP quests.
- **The Alliance Onyxia attunement does not start in Theramore.** It starts with [54] Dragonkin Menace from Helendis Riverhorn at Morgan's Vigil (Burning Steppes) and runs The True Masters → Marshal Windsor (BRD) → Jail Break! → The Great Masquerade → The Dragon's Eye → Drakefire Amulet. Theramore's headline Alliance chain is the end of The Missing Diplomat (Lady Jaina Proudmoore).

### Deeprun Tram
Stormwind (Dwarven District) ↔ Ironforge (Tinker Town). Free, ~60 s transit, in the game since launch. Use it whenever a route calls for "go to Ironforge" from Stormwind or vice versa (trainers, Hall of Explorers turn-ins, Tinker Town Gnomeregan quests).
