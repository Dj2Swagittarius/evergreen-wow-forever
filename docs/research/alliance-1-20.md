# Classic WoW (Classic Era / 1.15 data) – Alliance 1–20 Starting Routes

Research compiled 2026-09-19 from Joana's Classic Alliance web chapters (joanasworld.com), wow-pro Alliance guides (Joan's 13–20, Boston's 12–20, Caelestis's 12–20, Maw's 12–20), Sage Guide, Warcraft Tavern, warcraft.wiki.gg, Wowhead Classic, and (for WoW: Forever racials) Method, Icy Veins and Wowhead Forever.

Conventions
- Coordinates are zone map coordinates `(x,y)`. Coordinates without a tag were read directly from a fetched source. Anything marked **(verify)** could not be confirmed from a fetched page (from memory, or two sources disagreed) and should be checked in-game or on Wowhead Classic before it goes into the addon.
- Kill/collect counts: counts stated in fetched guides are given plainly; counts from memory are tagged (verify).
- "Leave at" is the level the guides recommend leaving the bracket, not a hard gate.
- Joana's 1–12/13 chapters were fetched in full. Joana's Classic 12–20 chapter (`joanasworld.com/classic/alliance-12-20.php`) is paywalled; the 12–20 brackets below are reconstructed from the free wow-pro/Sage/Warcraft Tavern routes plus wiki/Wowhead quest data. Note Joana's own Classic route sends every Alliance race to Darkshore at 12–17, then Loch Modan 17–18 and Redridge 18–20; the race-specific zone routes below follow the brief instead.

- **Hub:** Northshire Abbey (49,42). No inn, no flight point. Vendor/water: wagon outside the abbey (47,42) and supplies at (48,41).
- **Hearth:** none available until Goldshire (hearthstone is bound to Northshire Abbey by default at creation).

- **Class trainers:** inside the abbey (Warrior 50,42; Priest 50,39; Warlock 50,43). Warlocks need 1s10c for "Tainted Letter"/Summon Imp (50,43).

## D. Racial abilities

### D1. Classic Era (1.x / Classic Era 1.15) – verified values

Sources: Blizzard Watch Classic racial list; Wowhead Classic spell pages (Perception 20600, Stoneform 20594, Escape Artist 20589, Shadowmeld 20580).

**Human**
- Perception – "Dramatically increases stealth detection for 20 sec." Instant, 3-minute cooldown.
- The Human Spirit – Spirit increased by 5%.
- Diplomacy – Reputation gains increased by 10%.
- Sword Specialization – Skill with Swords and Two-Handed Swords increased by 5.
- Mace Specialization – Skill with Maces and Two-Handed Maces increased by 5.

**Dwarf**
- Stoneform – Grants immunity to Bleed, Poison and Disease effects; Armor increased by 10%. Lasts 8 sec. 3-minute cooldown. (Wowhead Classic's page lists the aura as "Mod Resistance % (Physical) 10%", i.e. the armor buff.)
- Gun Specialization – Guns skill increased by 5.
- Frost Resistance – Frost Resistance increased by 10.
- Find Treasure – Sense nearby treasure chests on the minimap (toggle).

**Gnome**
- Escape Artist – Escape the effects of any immobilization or movement-speed-reduction effect. 0.5 s cast, 1-minute cooldown.
- Expansive Mind – Intelligence increased by 5%.
- Arcane Resistance – Arcane Resistance increased by 10 (verify the number; Blizzard Watch's text omits it).
- Engineering Specialization – Engineering skill increased by 15.

**Night Elf**
- Shadowmeld – Enter stealth; lasts until cancelled or until you move. Out of combat only. 10-second cooldown (Wowhead Classic).
- Quickness – Dodge chance increased by 1%.
- Wisp Spirit – Become a wisp on death; movement speed while dead increased by 50%.
- Nature Resistance – Nature Resistance increased by 10.

### D2. WoW: Forever versions (announced 12–13 Sep 2026)

Sources: Method "All New Racial Abilities in World of Warcraft: Forever" (dated 13 Sep 2026); Icy Veins WoW Forever race guides (Human, Dwarf, Gnome, Night Elf); Wowhead Forever "All Racials and Available Class-Race Combinations" (updated 2026-09-16 – page body did not render for me, so its wording is **(verify)**). Design rule: every race gets 2 on-use + 2 passive racials; weapon-skill racials became crit racials.

**Human** – Will to Survive (new, active): removes all stun effects, 3-minute cooldown. Perception (active): dramatically increases stealth detection for 20 s, 3-minute cooldown (unchanged). Sword Specialization (passive): while wielding a Sword or Two-Handed Sword, spell and ability critical strike chance +2% (no more +5 weapon skill). The Human Spirit (passive): +5% Spirit (unchanged). Removed: Diplomacy, Mace Specialization. Human Hunter is a new combo. Priest-specific: Divine Grace, Feedback (Icy Veins).

**Dwarf** – Stoneform (active): removes and grants immunity to Bleed, Poison and Disease, and reduces Physical damage taken by 10% for 8 s, 3-minute cooldown (armor buff replaced by flat 10% physical DR). Find Treasure (active toggle): unchanged. Mace Specialization (passive): while wielding a Mace or Two-Handed Mace, spell and ability critical strike chance +1%. Big Game Hunter (new passive): damage to Beasts +5%. Removed: Gun Specialization, Frost Resistance.

**Gnome** – Escape Artist (active): removes movement-impairing effects and grants immunity to them for 3 s; no failure chance; 2-minute cooldown (up from 1 minute). Eureka! (new active): next 3 damaging or healing abilities cost 50% less mana and deal/heal 10% more; 2-minute cooldown. Expansive Mind (passive): maximum primary resource (Mana, Rage or Energy) +5% (was +5% Intellect). Engineering Specialization (passive): "engineering devices are more reliable" (was +15 skill). Removed: Arcane Resistance.

**Night Elf** – Elune's Light (new active): critical strike chance with all spells and attacks +10% for 15 s, 3-minute cooldown. Shadowmeld (active): stealth until you move; Rogues/Druids gain roughly +1 stealth level; usable in combat (drops threat), which puts it on a 2-minute cooldown. Quickness (passive): +1% Dodge and +2% run speed. Wisp Spirit (passive): +75% movement speed while dead (up from 50%). Removed: Nature Resistance.
