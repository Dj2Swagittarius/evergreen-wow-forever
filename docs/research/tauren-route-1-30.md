# Tauren route 1–30, Classic Era — research notes, Sep 19 2026

Sources: Joana's Mulgore 1–12 (public; 12–20 and 20–30 chapters are login-only), wow-pro Tauren starter 1–12, wow-pro Guide to The Barrens 12–20, Jame's Horde Chapter I 21–31, warcraft.wiki.gg zone quest lists and NPC pages, Warcraft Tavern, Wowhead Classic Horde guide. Mulgore coords agree across sources; Barrens/Stonetalon coords are from the Classic-era guides (wiki coords there are post-Cataclysm maps). Jame's sends Horde to Hillsbrad at 22–24 and 28; the 20–30 order below stays on Kalimdor (Joana's chapter order), which is our adaptation (verify in play).

Not in Era (skip): Kyle's Gone Missing! (added 2.1.2); Thousand Needles Wizlo/Elosai tonic chain (TBC). Bloodhoof Village has no flight master in Era (Tak is Cataclysm). Malaka'jin has no inn or flight master in Era.

## 1–6 Red Cloud Mesa
Camp Narache 45,77. Hearth bound there at creation. Grull Hawkwind 45,77: The Hunt Begins (7 meat, 7 feathers), The Hunt Continues (10 Mountain Cougar Pelts, hills S ~42,92), The Battleboars (8 snouts, 8 flanks). Chief Hawkwind 44,76: A Humble Task → Greatmother Hawkwind at the well 50,81 (Water Pitcher), Rites of the Earthmother → Seer Graytongue (hut SW ~42,92) → Rite of Strength (12 Bristleback Belts, Brambleblade Ravine cave 58,85, tent 62,78). Brave Windfeather: Break Sharptusk! (Chief Sharptusk Thornmantle in the tent 62,78). Dirt-stained Map in the cave → Attack on Camp Narache. Shaman: Call of Earth (Seer Ravenfeather). Then Rites of the Earthmother (→ Baine) and A Task Unfinished (Antur Fallow → Innkeeper Kauth). Leave at 6.

## Forever notes touching Tauren
Racials (Warcraft Tavern, Method, wow.gg, Icy Veins, Zockify): War Stomp 2 s stun up to 5 within 8 yd, 0.5 s cast, 2 min; Cultivation active: bonus herb node nobody needs Herbalism for; Plainsrunning passive: builds to +30% while moving, not in combat or instances (Icy Veins; beta hands-on agrees); Endurance +5% HP +1% hit. No new Tauren class (Blizzard Deep Dive lists the six new combos; forum thread has no blue post). Shen'dralas: Blizzard What's Next recap: "between Mulgore and Desolace", tied to the Shen'dralar, Eldre'Thalas, Dire Maul, centaur; lfcarry/mmojugg: entered from the Valley of Bones in southern Desolace; no level range anywhere (foreverchanges.pro: "not set yet"); wow.gg alone says Mulgore–Barrens. Mulgore/Barrens/TB: no documented changes; beta blog notes new lighting and Hyjal visible from north Mulgore. Dates: Blizzard: beta Sep 17, launch Nov 4 2026 3 pm PST.
