# Classic Era (1.15) Class Quest Chains, Levels 1-60

Scope: every class-quest chain NOT already covered in `tauren-class-quests.md` (Tauren) or the Undead guide, i.e. all Alliance races, plus Orc/Troll where their version differs from Tauren/Undead, plus what is publicly known about the WoW: Forever (Nov 2026) new race/class combos. Data is Classic Era; Forever beta (level cap 30 as of 16 Sep 2026) is noted separately.

Verification method: chain structure and quest levels come from warcraft.wiki.gg class-quest index pages and per-quest pages; NPC names/objectives/rewards from those pages plus Icy Veins, Millenium and Wowhead Classic quest pages; coordinates as shown on the wiki NPC/quest pages or Icy Veins/noobtoboss walkthroughs. Anything I could not confirm from a page I actually loaded is tagged **(verify)**. Coordinates are `x, y` on the zone map.

Format per quest: **Name** [level] — Giver (zone, coords) → Objective (coords) → Turn-in → Reward.

## WARRIOR

### Alliance — Level 10: Defensive Stance (also teaches Taunt + Sunder Armor r1)

Every race: pick up the trainer breadcrumb at 10, go to a second NPC, kill/collect, return. Chain is race-specific; the final weapon-reward sub-quests are optional.

**Human (Elwynn Forest / Stormwind)**
1. **A Warrior's Training** [10] — warrior trainer (Lyria Du Lac, Goldshire, Lion's Pride Inn **(verify giver)**) → speak with Harry Burlguard, Pig and Whistle Tavern, Old Town, Stormwind (76.1, 53.3).
2. **Bartleby the Drunk** [10] — Harry Burlguard → speak with Bartleby (same tavern). 85 XP.
3. **Beat Bartleby** [10] — Bartleby → duel Bartleby and beat him (do not kill; he yields) → Bartleby.
4. **Bartleby's Mug** [10] — Bartleby → return his mug to Harry Burlguard → **Defensive Stance, Taunt, Sunder Armor (r1)**.
5. **Marshal Haggard** [10] — Harry Burlguard → speak with Marshal Haggard, Eastvale Logging Camp, Elwynn (≈ 82, 67 **(verify coords)**). 420 XP.
6. **Dead-tooth Jack** [10] — Marshal Haggard → retrieve Haggard's Badge from Dead-tooth Jack's camp (east Elwynn, NE of Eastvale **(verify coords)**) → Haggard → choice: Haggard's Axe / Hammer / Dagger / Sword.

**Dwarf & Gnome (Dun Morogh / Ironforge)**
1. **Muren Stormpike** [10] — trainer → speak with Muren Stormpike, Hall of Arms, Military Ward, Ironforge.
2. **Vejrek** [11] — Muren Stormpike → kill Vejrek (Frostmane troll, Dun Morogh 27, 57, south of Frostmane Hold), bring Vejrek's Head → Muren → **Defensive Stance, Taunt, Sunder Armor (r1)**.
3. **Tormus Deepforge** [11] — Muren Stormpike → speak with Tormus Deepforge (Ironforge, The Great Forge **(verify district)**).
4. **Ironband's Compound** [11] — Tormus Deepforge → loot a load of Umbral Ore from the chest at Ironband's Compound, east Dun Morogh, south of Helm's Bed Lake (77, 61); guarded by Captain Beld (lvl 11) + Dark Iron dwarves → Tormus. 650 XP.
5. **Grey Iron Weapons** [10] — Tormus → he forges; choose a Grey Iron weapon (axe/sword/mace/etc. **(verify exact list)**).

**Night Elf (Teldrassil / Darnassus)**
1. **Elanaria** [10] — trainer → speak with Elanaria, Warrior's Terrace, Darnassus.
2. **Vorlus Vilehoof** [10] — Elanaria → kill the satyr Vorlus Vilehoof (lvl 10) at the remote moonwell SE of Ban'ethil Barrow Den, Teldrassil; torch-lined path starts at 49, 59 → bring Horn of Vorlus to Elanaria → 850 XP, **Defensive Stance, Taunt, Sunder Armor (r1)**.
3. **The Shade of Elura** [10] — Elanaria → 8 Elunite Ore + Elura's Medallion from the shipwreck south of the Auberdine dock, Darkshore (31, 44; crates scattered east of ship and under the dock; Shade of Elura is the ghost there) → Elanaria. 850 XP.

**Orc/Troll (The Barrens)** — differs from Tauren only in breadcrumb: **Veteran Uzzek** [10] → **Path of Defense** [10] (Uzzek, Barrens road near Far Watch Post: collect Flatland Cougar/Savannah Prowler? claws **(verify objective)**) → **Thun'grim Firegaze** [10] → **Forged Steel** [10] (weapon).

### Forever: Undead (Forsaken) Paladin — beta info as of Sep 2026
- Horde paladin via Undead only. Starts in Deathknell with class-specific intro quests **"Rediscovering the Light"** (heal injured Deathguards with Holy Light) and **"A Light in the Darkness"** (rescue in Night's Web Hollow) — names from an mmoexp beta write-up **(verify in-client)**.
- Around level 10 the class hub is **Bandarion Keep, Whispering Wood, Tirisfal Glades** ("most of their class quests come from the keep"). No quest names, NPCs or coordinates for Redemption/Sense Undead/Warhorse equivalents have been published; guides explicitly say Blizzard is withholding them **(verify)**.
- **Holy Strike** is baseline for ALL paladins at level 6: instant Holy weapon strike (25% weapon dmg + Holy), 12-s CD. Consecration baseline at 20; Seal of Fury = Protection taunt; Judgement no longer consumes the seal.
- Level 60 epic mount: Forsaken get a Retribution-themed charger chain (skeletal horse / skull-helmed ram in gold plate); steps unpublished **(verify)**.

**Orc/Troll — Thotar, Razor Hill, Durotar** (same three as the Horde Tauren doc but different mobs): Dire Mottled Boar (near Razor Hill) → Surf Crawler (coast by Sen'jin/Tiragarde **(verify)**) → Armored Scorpid (Drygulch Ravine **(verify)**) → **Training the Beast** → Ormak Grimshot, Orgrimmar.
