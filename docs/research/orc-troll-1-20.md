# Orc & Troll 1–20, Classic Era (1.15) — research notes, Sep 19 2026

Durotar (Valley of Trials → Sen'jin Village → Razor Hill → Orgrimmar) then the Barrens from the Durotar side. Coordinates are Classic zone-map coords unless flagged. **(verify)** = only one fan-guide source, a post-Cataclysm wiki coordinate, or not confirmed at all. Quest levels are shown as [quest level, requires level] where known.

Sourcing notes:
- Objective counts for the Valley of Trials starter quests were taken from **Wowhead Classic** quest pages. warcraft.wiki.gg serves the post-Cataclysm versions for those (6 boars / 8 tails / 6 apples / 7 felstalkers); the Classic Era numbers are 10 / 10 / 10 / medallion only.
- Joana's and wow-pro refused verbatim extraction, so only individual facts (coords, counts) were pulled from them and cross-checked against Wowhead / wiki.
- warcraft.wiki.gg NPC pages give retail coordinates; for NPCs that did not move in Cataclysm (Valley of Trials, Sen'jin, Razor Hill, Durotar interior) they match Classic. Barrens coords from the wiki are retail "Northern Barrens" coords and were replaced with Classic-map coords from the project's Tauren research where available.

## 2. Levels 6–12 — Sen'jin Village, Razor Hill, the rest of Durotar

### Sen'jin Village 56,75 (most guides do not bind here; bind Razor Hill)

### Loops

**Echo Isles loop (7–9)** — south-east of Sen'jin: tigers (Practical Prey), taillashers (Break a Few Eggs), crawlers/makrura on the beaches (A Solvent Spirit), skull shrine 67,88, Zalazane's camp 67,86 (Voodoo/Hexed trolls level 8–10), Ju-Ju Heaps for mages. Do it once at 8–9 so Zalazane is one pull.

**Tiragarde Keep loop (7–9)** — 57,55: sailors and marines outside and in the courtyard, Benedict upstairs, chest behind him. Canvas Scraps drop here. Then swim the Scuttle Coast wreckage 61,52 for Gnomish Tools and kill crawlers/makrura in the same water for A Solvent Spirit.

**Razormane loop (9–10)** — Razormane Grounds south of Razor Hill (Quilboar, Scouts) for Encroachment part one; Kolkar Crag SW of Sen'jin for the Attack Plans + Canvas Scraps.

**Drygulch Ravine / Razorwind Canyon (9–11)** — north of Razor Hill on the Orgrimmar road. **Rezlak** (goblin, Tinkers' Union) 46.4,23.0 by the road: **Winds in the Desert** [9] — **5 Sacks of Supplies** from Dustwind harpies in Razorwind Canyon (~49.5,22); → **Securing the Lines** [11] — **12 Dustwind Savages, 8 Dustwind Storm Witches** in Drygulch Ravine (~51–54,23–27). These are the only neutral quests in Durotar.

**Dustwind Cave / northern Razormane (10–11)** — Razormane Dustrunners and Battleguards camp between Drygulch Ravine and the coast (~54,27 · 56,27) for Encroachment part two. Dustwind Cave itself is a harpy cave in the same cliffs (coords verify); nothing sends you inside except the Securing the Lines counts.

**Thunder Ridge (10–12)** — NW Durotar, entrance ~39,31. Thunder Lizards and Lightning Hides (9–11) for the warrior quest; **Fizzle Darkstorm** at 42.1,26.6 for Dark Storms. **Misha Tor'kren** at Tor'kren Farm 43.1,30.2 on the way: **Lost But Not Forgotten** [11] — Kron's Amulet is a random drop from **Dreadmaw Crocolisks** along the **Southfury River** (west edge of Durotar, ~36–40,30–45 verify).

**Southfury River (10–12)** — crocolisks for Kron's Amulet; **Rhinag** at Rocktusk Farm (NW Durotar, ~41,18 verify): **Need for a Cure** [9, req 7] — fetch the Venomtail Antidote from **Kor'ghan** in the Cleft of Shadow 47,53, whose **Finding the Antidote** wants **4 Venomtail Poison Sacs** from Venomtail Scorpids (level 9–10, south-west of the Orgrimmar gate). Reward Charging Buckler or Light Scorpid Armor. Also the shaman **Shrine of the Dormant Flame** (Telf Joolam) sits atop the peak by the river (see §5).

**Skull Rock (11–12)** — cave in the NE cliffs at ~55,10 (wow-pro Cata coords 55.0,9.8; Joana 55,10). **Margoz** [12] — Orgnil → Margoz at his tent south of Bladefist Bay 56.5,20.1 → **Skull Rock** [12]: **6 Searing Collars** from Burning Blade cultists in the cave. **Gazz'uz** (level 14 warlock, back of the cave) drops the **Eye of Burning Shadow** → **Burning Shadows** [12] → Neeru Fireblade in the Cleft of Shadow. Also in the cave: the **Burning Blade Stash** (Tablet of Verga, warlock Creature of the Void) and the **Lieutenant's Insignia** drop for Thrall's Hidden Enemies. → **Neeru Fireblade** [12] (Margoz → Neeru) → **Ak'Zeloth** [13] (Neeru → Ak'Zeloth at Far Watch Post) → **The Demon Seed** [14, req 9]: take the Flawed Power Stone to the Altar of Fire just below the summit of **Dreadmist Peak 48,18** (Barrens, NW of the Crossroads; the altar is in the small cavern in front of the Burning Blade camp, not the top platform) within 30 min. Reward Banshee Armor.

## 3. Levels 12–20 — The Barrens, entering from Durotar

The quest content is the same as the Tauren route (see `tauren.html` "12–20 The Barrens" and `tauren-class-quests.md`); the differences for an Orc/Troll are the entry road and hearth/trainer logistics.

**The road in.** Razor Hill → west road → bridge over the Southfury → **Far Watch Post** (Classic map ~62,35, verify; retail wiki says 67,39 / 66,42). Kargal Battlescar (Conscript of the Horde → Crossroads Conscription), **Ak'Zeloth** (Ak'Zeloth turn-in, The Demon Seed), **Uzzek** under the tree SW of the post (warrior Veteran Uzzek / Path of Defense / Thun'grim Firegaze). Continue west; the road bends south to the Crossroads 52,30. Shamans stop at **Grol'dom Farm** (Kranal Fiss, Classic ~56,20 verify) on the way for Call of Fire.

**Hearth / trainers.** Orgrimmar is the trainer city: fly Crossroads → Orgrimmar (Devrak) at 14 and 18 (Tauren fly to Thunder Bluff instead). Hearth at the Crossroads inn (Boorand Plainswind) until ~16, then Camp Taurajo (Innkeeper Byula). **Apothecary Zamah** (Helbrim → Thunder Bluff) is optional for non-Tauren; skip unless you want the TB flight point.

**Crossroads sweep (12–13).** Sergra Darkthorn 52,31: **Plainstrider Menace** (7 Plainstrider Beaks) → **The Zhevra** (4 Zhevra Hooves) → **Prowlers of the Barrens** (7 Prowler Claws, Savannah Prowlers ~60,35) → **Echeyakee** (blow the horn at the den 55,16, kill the white lion, loot the hide) → **The Angry Scytheclaws**. Thork 51,30: **Disrupt the Attacks** (8 Razormane Water Seekers, 8 Thornweavers, 3 Hunters, Thorn Hill) → **The Disruption Ends** (Kreenig Snarlsnout at the Razormane camp 58,24); **Supplies for the Crossroads** (5 crates at the wrecked caravans on the roads). Gazrog: **Raptor Thieves** (12 Raptor Heads) → later **Stolen Silver**. Apothecary Helbrim: **Fungal Spores** (4, beasts at the Forgotten Pools). Tonga Runetotem: **The Forgotten Pools** (bubbling fissure at the pools 45,30) → **The Stagnant Oasis** → Altered Beings (6 Altered Snapjaw Shells at 55,42). Mankrik: **Lost in Battle** (the Beaten Corpse south of the road at 51,50) → **Consumed by Hatred** (60 Bristleback Quilboar Tusks — passive, finishes at 18–20 at the Bristleback camps north of Taurajo). Darsok Swiftdagger: **Harpy Raiders** (8 Witchwing Talons, Dry Hills NW) → **Harpy Lieutenants** → **Serena Bloodfeather**. Regthar Deathgate's bunker 45,28: **Centaur Bracers** (15) and **Kolkar Leaders** (Barak Kodobane, 16 elite, west of the Forgotten Pools) → **Verog the Dervish** (spawns from Kolkar kills at the Stagnant Oasis 53,41) → **Hezrul Bloodmark**.

**Ratchet 63,37 (13–14).** Bragok FP. Gazlowe: **Southsea Freebooters** (12 Southsea Brigands, 6 Cannoneers on the coast south) → **The Missing Shipment** (Wharfmaster Dizzywig → Gilthares) → **Stolen Booty**; **WANTED: Baron Longshore** (poster). Sputtervalve: **Samophlange** chain (Control Console at the Sludge Fen 52,12, three valves, Tinkerer Sniggles in the hut 53,11, then Wenikee Boltbucket at Mor'shan Rampart 50,11 → Rilli Greasygob in Orgrimmar → Boss Copperplug, Boulder Lode Mine, for the manual cover). ## 4. Orc and Troll racials

### Classic Era (1.15) — verified against Icy Veins Classic race guides and warcraft.wiki.gg
**Orc**
- **Blood Fury** (active, 2 min CD): +25% base melee attack power for 15 s, healing received reduced by 50% for 25 s.
- **Hardiness** (passive): +25% chance to fully resist stun effects.
- **Command** (passive): pet damage +5% (hunter / warlock pets).
- **Axe Specialization** (passive): +5 weapon skill with one-handed and two-handed axes.

**Troll**
- **Berserking** (active, 3 min CD): attack and casting speed +10% to +30% for 10 s, scaling with missing health (10% at full, 30% at ≤40% health).
- **Regeneration** (passive): health regeneration +10%, and 10% of normal regeneration continues in combat.
- **Beast Slaying** (passive): +5% damage against Beasts.
- **Bow Specialization** (passive): +5 weapon skill with bows.
- **Throwing Specialization** (passive): +5 weapon skill with thrown weapons.

### WoW Forever (Classic+) — announced Sep 12 2026 (Warcraft Tavern, from the BlizzCon demo), Method Sep 13, Icy Veins Forever race guides, Wowhead Forever guide dated 2026-09-16
Every race now has two actives + two passives.

**Orc**
- **Blood Fury** (active, 2 min CD): +10% Attack Power **and** Spell Power for 15 s. No healing penalty any more.
- **Shatter Curse** (new active, 3 min CD): removes and grants immunity to Curses and Banes, and reduces magical damage taken by 15% for 8 s.
- **Axe Specialization** (passive, reworked): while wielding an axe or two-handed axe, +1% spell and ability critical strike chance (crit is a unified stat in Forever).
- **Hardiness** (passive, reworked): stun durations reduced by 20% (was 25% resist chance).
- **Command** removed.
- Classes: Hunter, **Mage (new)**, Rogue, Shaman, Warlock, Warrior.

**Troll**
- **Berserking** (active, 3 min CD): +10% attack and casting speed for 10 s, flat (no longer scales with missing health).
- **Rapid Regeneration** (new active, 3 min CD): channel to restore 50% of maximum health over 6 s; moving, acting or taking damage cancels it.
- **Beast Slaying** (passive): +5% damage to Beasts (unchanged).
- **Regeneration** (passive): +10% health regeneration and 10% continues in combat (unchanged).
- **Bow Specialization / Throwing Specialization** removed.
- Classes: Hunter, Mage, Priest, Rogue, Shaman, **Warlock (new)**, Warrior.

Icy Veins' Forever troll page still lists the priest racial spells Hex of Weakness and Shadowguard with Classic wording (verify whether Forever keeps the level-10/20 priest quests).

Cooldowns on Blood Fury (2 min) and Shatter Curse / Berserking / Rapid Regeneration (3 min) are from Icy Veins and the Wowhead Forever summary; the Warcraft Tavern/Method demo notes do not list cooldowns. The wowhead.com/forever guide body could not be fetched (JS-rendered); only its date was confirmed.

## 5. Orc / Troll class-quest NPCs that differ from the Tauren route

### Hunter — Thotar, Razor Hill 52,43
1. **The Hunter's Path** [10] — any Horde hunter trainer (Jen'shan in the valley, Thotar himself) → speak with Thotar.
2. **Taming the Beast** [10] — tame a **Dire Mottled Boar** (right outside Razor Hill).

5. **Training the Beast** [10] — speak with **Ormak Grimshot**, Hunter's Hall, Valley of Honor, Orgrimmar 66,18.5 → Beast Training (pet skills). Wowhead IDs: 6062 → 6083 → 6082 → 6081 (boar / crawler / scorpid order per the quest text: the scorpid is "the final creature").

### Shaman
- **Call of Earth** [4] ×3 — Canaga Earthcaller 42.4,69.2, Valley of Trials: 2 Felstalker Hooves → Hidden Path 41,73 to Spirit Rock 44,76, drink Earth Sapta → Rough Quartz back to Canaga. Reward Earth Totem + Stoneskin Totem. (Tauren version is Seer Ravenfeather / Red Cloud Mesa.)
- **Call of Fire** [10] ×5 — **Searn Firewarder**, Grommash Hold, Valley of Wisdom, Orgrimmar (Wowhead 1522; Xanis Flameweaver 1523 / Swart 2984 / Narm Skychaser 2983 are the Thunder Bluff / Durotar / Mulgore starts) → **Kranal Fiss**, Grol'dom Farm, Barrens (Classic ~56,20 verify; retail 56,40) → (1524) carry the Torch of the Dormant Flame to **Telf Joolam** at the **Shrine of the Dormant Flame**, top of the peak by the Southfury River NW of the Valley of Trials, Durotar (~37,58 verify; path up starts at the river's SW tip) → (1525) **1 Fire Tar** (Razormane Mystics/Geomancers, Barrens quilboar camps) + **1 Reagent Pouch** (Burning Blade Cultists — Wowhead says "cave north-east of Razor Hill", i.e. Skull Rock; verify vs the coven cave) → reward Fire Sapta → (1526) drink it at the shrine, kill the **Minor Manifestation of Fire**, put the Glowing Ember in the brazier → Torch of the Eternal Flame → (1527) back to Kranal Fiss. Reward **Fire Totem + Searing Totem**. Best done at 12–13 on the walk to the Crossroads.
- Call of Water [20] (Islen Waterseer south of Ratchet ~66,44 verify) and Call of Air [30] start from Searn Firewarder as well.

### Warlock
- **Imp [4]** — Ruzan, Valley of Trials (west of the Den entrance): Vile Familiars (warlock) — 6 Vile Familiar Heads → Summon Imp.
- **Voidwalker [10–11]** — **Gan'rul's Summons** [10] from **Ophek**, Razor Hill 54,41 → **Gan'rul Bloodeye**, Darkfire Enclave, Cleft of Shadow ~47,50 → **Creature of the Void** [11]: loot the **Tablet of Verga** from the **Burning Blade Stash** (chest) inside Skull Rock ~55,10 → **The Binding** [11]: use the Glyphs of Summoning at the summoning circle in **Neeru Fireblade's tent** in the Cleft, defeat the voidwalker, return the glyphs → **Summon Voidwalker**. (Zureetha's Vile Familiars / Burning Blade Medallion are ordinary quests, not the voidwalker chain.)
- **Succubus [20]** — **Devourer of Souls** (Gan'rul → Cazul, the blind warlock in the Cleft of Shadow) → **Blind Cazul** (Cazul → Zankaja 37,60 Orgrimmar, between the Valley of Wisdom and Valley of Strength) → **News of Dogran** (Zankaja → Ken'zigla, Malaka'jin, Stonetalon 73,95) → **News of Dogran (2)** (→ Grunt Logmar, Camp Taurajo) → **Ken'zigla's Draught** → **Dogran's Captivity** (deliver the draught to Grunt Dogran, held in the two-hut quilboar camp north of Camp Taurajo 43,49; heavily guarded) → **Love's Gift** (→ Zankaja) → **The Binding (succubus)** (Cazul; summon and defeat her) → **Summon Succubus**. Takar the Seer east of Taurajo is the *Alliance* Devourer of Souls / Heartswood / What Is Love? NPC — Horde do not use him.
- **Felhunter [30]** — **Seeking Strahad** (Gan'rul Bloodeye in Orgrimmar or Carendin Halgar in Undercity) → **Strahad Farsan** in the tower at Ratchet ~63,37 → **Tome of the Cabal (Horde)** ×3 (first step: speak with Jorah Annison, cartographer, Undercity; later steps verify) → **The Binding (felhunter)** at Strahad's circle → Summon Felhunter.

### Warrior — Defensive Stance
- **Veteran Uzzek** [10] — Tarshaw Jaggedscar, Razor Hill Barracks 54,43 (Sorek in Orgrimmar and Krang Stonehoof in Bloodhoof also give it) → **Uzzek**, under the tree just south-west of Far Watch Post, Barrens (~62,31 verify; retail 66,42).
- **Path of Defense** [10] — Uzzek: **5 Singed Scales** from Thunder Lizards / Lightning Hides at **Thunder Ridge**, Durotar (cross the bridge back east, ridge entrance ~39,31). Reward: **Defensive Stance** (+ Sunder Armor / Taunt per Icy Veins and the Tauren notes; verify which spells the quest itself grants).
- → **Thun'grim Firegaze** (Thorn Hill 57,30) → **Forged Steel** (Stolen Iron Chest by the broken wagon 55,26) → Thun'grim's Axe/Mace/Dagger/Sword. Identical to the Tauren chain from step 2.

### Rogue — Poisons at 20
- **The Shattered Salute** [20] — **Shenthul**, Shadowswift Brotherhood, Cleft of Shadow 43,53.7: watch his salute, then /salute him. (The wiki lists Rogues of the Shattered Hand [24] / Find the Shattered Hand before it — verify whether those gate it in 1.15.)
- **Deep Cover** [20] — Shenthul: at the Venture Co. tower, Sludge Fen, NE Barrens, fire the Flare Gun twice, then /salute **Taskmaster Fizzule** so he turns friendly.
- **Mission: Possible But Not Probable** [21] — Fizzule: kill 2 each **Mutated Venture Co. Drone, Venture Co. Lookout, Venture Co. Patroller**; **pickpocket Silixiz's Tower Key** from Foreman Silixiz; kill **Grand Foreman Puzik Gallywix** (23 elite, group) and take his head; **lockpick Gallywix's Lockbox** (needs ~75 Lockpicking) for the Cache of Zanzil's Altered Mixture → **Shenthul**. Sources disagree on exactly which turn-in teaches **Poisons**: Wowhead lists Recipe: Thistle Tea on this quest and "Hinott's Assistance" (Serge Hinott, Tarren Mill) as the follow-up; Icy Veins/Warcraft Tavern put Poisons at the end of the chain. Treat as: Poisons is unlocked at Shenthul when this chain completes (verify in-game).
- No Horde rogue class quest at 10.

### Priest — Troll racial spells (quests, not just training)
- **Hex of Weakness** [10] — quest from **Ken'jai** (Valley of Trials) or **Tai'jin** (Razor Hill Barracks 54,43): speak with **Ur'kyo**, Spirit Lodge, Valley of Spirits, Orgrimmar 35.6,87.7 → learn Hex of Weakness (rank 1: target's damage −2 and healing received −20% for 2 min, 35 mana; 6 ranks to 60).
- **Shadowguard** [20] — quest from **Zayus / Ur'kyo** in the Valley of Spirits (also offered in Thunder Bluff / Undercity versions): learn Shadowguard (rank 1: 3 charges, 10 min, 20 Shadow damage per charge to attackers, no threat, 50 mana; 7 ranks).
- Ken'jai also gives Hallowed Tablet [1] / In Favor of Spirituality [4]; Tai'jin gives Garments of Spirituality.

### Mage
- **Ju-Ju Heaps** [10, Troll mage] — Un'thuwa, Sen'jin Village mage trainer: destroy 4 Ju-Ju Heaps among the huts on the main Echo Isle → Ley Orb or Ley Staff. Nothing else until the level-30 wand chain.
